Yes. Different customer groups respond better to personalized offers and recommendations.

Can WhatsApp campaigns increase Diwali jewellery sales?

Absolutely. Personalized engagement and timely follow-ups often improve conversions.

How can CRM improve Diwali campaigns?

CRM helps businesses track customer interactions, segment audiences, personalize communication, and manage leads.

Campaign reach, engagement, appointments, conversions, revenue, and repeat purchases.
