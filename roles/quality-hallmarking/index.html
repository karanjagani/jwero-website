QUALITY & HALLMARKING QUESTIONS

Straight answers about certification, tracking and compliance.

What does Jwero do for a jewellery quality and hallmarking staff member?

Jwero gives quality and hallmarking staff a hallmarking screen that tracks batches sent and returned, with the hallmark ID recorded on each piece and printed on tags and bills. Goods received from suppliers pass QC before entering stock, and the manufacturing QC station records a verdict on each stage and finished batch, with a reason required for every fail.

Does Jwero perform hallmarking or assaying itself?

No. Jwero tracks documentation and status, it does not perform the physical hallmarking or certification testing process. The actual assaying and certification still happens wherever it does today.

Can it track which pieces are pending certification versus cleared?

Yes, a piece’s documentation status is tracked as part of its job record, so you can see what’s pending versus cleared without chasing paper across departments.

Does it handle statutory compliance filing for me?

Not as a filing system, it tracks the documentation trail tied to a job so you have the record on hand; the statutory process and filing itself remain your responsibility.

Can I pass a whole batch with one click?

No, by design. Verdicts are recorded one work order at a time through a dialog with no preselected outcome, and a failing verdict must carry a reason.

Does Jwero fetch hallmark ID or certificates automatically?

No. You record the hallmark ID and certificate details against each piece or stone, and Jwero then shows and searches them. It does not pull data from the hallmarking centre or labs.

Will the owner see who passed a faulty piece?

Yes. QC verdicts and their reasons are recorded against the work order, so the owner can see who decided what. The same record shows every time you failed a piece and why, which protects your judgement.
