A DAY IN THIS ROLE

What actually changes when stock is the job.

TodayWith Jwero
Knowing what’s not movingSee Inventory →

TodayA piece can sit in the case for months before anyone notices, it takes a yearly stocktake or a sharp memory to catch it.

With JweroAgeing bands (0–30, 31–90, 91–180, 180+ days) and fast/slow-mover views surface exactly what’s sitting and for how long, checkable any day, not once a year.

Valuing dead stock at today’s rate

TodayKnowing what frozen capital is actually costing means pulling weight and purity records and repricing them by hand against today’s rate.

With JweroAgeing pieces are valued continuously at the live gold rate, so the frozen-capital number is always current, not a stale estimate.

Keeping branch stock counts honest

TodayEach branch keeps its own count; reconciling what a branch says it has against what’s actually there means phone calls and spreadsheet cross-checks.

With JweroStock, transfers and valuation are visible per branch and roll up centrally on the same record, so a discrepancy shows up without a round of calls.

Staying in sync with online sales

TodayA piece sells on the Shopify storefront but still shows available in the in-store count until someone manually updates it.

With JweroShopify/WooCommerce/Unicommerce connectors sync stock and orders both ways, so an online sale reflects in-store immediately.

WHAT THIS ROLE GAINS

Skills that move the job from counting to deciding.

Reading ageing data to make real reorder decisions

Working from ageing bands and mover data instead of a gut sense of “this looks old” builds the habit of deciding with a number, not a hunch.

Dead-stock triage instead of a once-a-year surprise

Catching slow movers at 90 days instead of at the annual stocktake means markdowns, melting or transfers happen while there’s still value to protect.

Cross-branch stock fluency

A centrally visible, branch-aware stock record builds comfort managing inventory across locations, not just the one you can physically walk.

Omnichannel stock discipline

Managing a count that syncs with Shopify, WooCommerce or Unicommerce builds the habit of treating online and in-store as one number, not two.

Jwero does not forecast demand or generate a reorder plan, that’s explicitly not built yet. The decision, markdown, melt, transfer, hold, reorder, stays a judgment call for a person who knows the season. See why this is true for every role at Jwero →

HOW TO CONTRIBUTE MORE

Concrete moves, not vague advice.

  1. 1

    Check ageing bands weekly, not just at stocktake

    Review the 91–180 and 180+ day bands on a set schedule so a slow mover gets a decision before it’s a write-off conversation.

  2. 2

    Cross-check branch stock centrally before a physical count

    Use the rolled-up branch view to flag discrepancies ahead of a stocktake, so the physical count confirms rather than discovers.

  3. 3

    Treat online and in-store as one number

    Use the Shopify/WooCommerce/Unicommerce sync as the source of truth for availability, instead of maintaining a separate mental count for online orders.

  4. 4

    Pair ageing data with your own seasonal knowledge

    Since demand forecasting isn’t automated, use the ageing and mover visibility as an input to your own judgment on what to reorder or discount, not a final answer.

INVENTORY QUESTIONS

Dead stock, forecasting, and staying in sync online.

Can Jwero tell me my dead stock?

Yes, ageing bands (0–30, 31–90, 91–180, 180+ days) and fast/slow-mover views show exactly which pieces are sitting, for how long, and what they’re worth at today’s rate.

Does it forecast demand or tell me what to order?

Not yet, predictive demand forecasting is on the roadmap, not shipped. What Jwero gives you today is visibility: ageing, valuation and mover data you use to make the reorder call yourself, not a prediction it makes for you.

Does this replace our physical stocktake?

No, but it makes it faster and less of a surprise-finding exercise, ageing and valuation are visible continuously, so a stocktake confirms what you already suspected rather than revealing it cold.

Does inventory stay in sync if we also sell on Shopify or Unicommerce?

Yes, Shopify, WooCommerce and Unicommerce connectors sync stock and orders both ways, so a piece sold online doesn’t sit as available in your in-store count.
