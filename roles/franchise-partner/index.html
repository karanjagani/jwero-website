FRANCHISE PARTNER QUESTIONS

Control, independence and consistency: answered.

What does Jwero do for a jewellery franchise partner?

Jwero gives a franchise outlet its own counter, stock and customer records inside the brand's account. You bill at the brand's rate and making rules, receive stock from head office by tracked transfer, use the brand's ready posts and templates, and train your staff on courses head office creates. Head office sees your sales and stock from the same billing.

Does head office control everything, or do I still run my branch my way?

Central price rules and brand catalogue sit under the franchisor’s control, but day-to-day running of your branch stays yours, staff, floor operations and local exceptions route through an approval, they’re not taken away from you.

If a customer from another franchise branch walks into mine, will I know who they are?

Yes, the customer record is shared across branches in the network, so their purchase history and preferences show up at your counter too, not just the one they usually visit.

Can I ever price or promote something differently from the rest of the network?

Local exceptions are possible, but they route through the franchisor’s approval rather than happening silently, brand consistency stays intact while giving you a channel to flag what your branch specifically needs.

Am I just becoming an operator with no real decisions left to make?

No, the system standardises pricing, catalogue and brand consistency, which is exactly the admin overhead most franchisees want off their plate. Local judgment, staffing, customer relationships, day-to-day floor decisions, stays with you.

What can head office see about my outlet?

Head office sees your outlet's sales and stock from the billing you do in Jwero, through the franchise screen and reports. What your staff can open is set by the roles given to them.

Can I set my own discounts?

Discount limits come from the rules set in Jwero. Discounts above the limit wait for approval before the bill goes through, and each approval is logged with who gave it.

Can I use my own WhatsApp number for my customers?

Chats come into a shared inbox per account. Whether you can connect a separate number depends on how the WhatsApp Business accounts are set up, so this is checked with you and head office before you start.
