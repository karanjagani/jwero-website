SALES ASSOCIATE QUESTIONS

Straight answers about the role and the change.

What does Jwero do for a jewellery salesperson?

Jwero helps a jewellery salesperson serve the customer in front of them. Scan a tag and the price fills in at today's rate. Her history, occasions and past visits show while she is in the store. You can send a catalogue on WhatsApp, give pieces on trial, follow up walk-outs, and see your own incentives and targets on your phone.

Will I stop talking to customers because AI handles the chat?

No, AI staff draft replies and follow-ups; a person on your team, often you, approves before anything reaches a customer. The conversations that matter, in-store, on a call, closing a sale, stay yours.

Do I need to remember every customer myself?

No, that’s the point of the customer record. Occasions, scheme balances and taste preferences are one tap away, so you walk up already knowing her instead of relying on memory.

What happens to my personal WhatsApp customer relationships if I leave?

The conversation history and customer record live on the business’s system, not a personal phone, which protects the business, and also means a new associate can pick up context instead of starting cold.

Will AI take credit for sales I influenced?

The action log records what AI drafted and who approved it, so a sale that started as an AI-drafted reply you approved and then closed in person is visible as your work, not hidden inside a bot.

Will the AI take my job?

No. The AI assistant answers routine WhatsApp questions within limits the owner sets, and hands chats to staff in the inbox. Showing jewellery, building trust and closing the sale stay with you; the AI handles repeat questions like timings.

Will the owner see every customer I bring in?

Yes. Walk-ins, outcomes and sales are recorded against the salesperson, which is also how your conversions and incentives are counted. Customer records belong to the business, so any colleague can serve her when you are away.

Is it hard to learn?

Scan the tag and the bill fills in with weight, purity, rate and making. Customer lookup and catalogues are a few taps. Your manager can also assign short training courses you open from your own learning page.
