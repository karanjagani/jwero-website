E-COMMERCE MANAGER QUESTIONS

Straight answers about this role.

Do I have to leave Shopify or WooCommerce?

No, Jwero connects to Shopify, WooCommerce and Unicommerce and syncs stock and orders both ways. It adds channels and live-rate pricing on top of the storefront you already run; it doesn’t replace it.

Does stock sync automatically between my store and Jwero?

Yes, the Shopify/WooCommerce/Unicommerce connectors sync inventory and orders both ways, so a sale on either side reflects everywhere without manual reconciliation.

Can my storefront show live gold-rate pricing?

Jwero’s catalogue and pricing follow the live metal rate, something a generic ecommerce platform doesn’t do natively. That pricing logic sits on top of your connected storefront.

Will this replace my role managing the storefront?

No, Jwero handles the connections, memory and messaging layer around your storefront. Merchandising decisions, storefront design and channel strategy stay with you; AI only drafts customer-facing messages for your approval.

Can I list on Google Shopping from Jwero?

Not yet. Jwero connects to Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon and Flipkart, but Google Shopping sync is not available today, so listings there are managed outside Jwero.

Do marketplace orders come in automatically?

Yes. Once Amazon and Flipkart are connected, their orders come into Jwero as sales orders, and store orders land in one order list with payment and status.

Will the AI chat agent promise customers things I cannot deliver?

You set what the assistant may do alone and what needs approval, teach it from a knowledge base, and every reply is kept in logs. Staff see every conversation in the shared inbox.
