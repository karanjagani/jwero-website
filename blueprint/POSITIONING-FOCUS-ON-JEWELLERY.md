## To supply or decide before this goes live

1. **Managed services are described but not priced.** The pages say managed work is "scoped and quoted". Decide the commercial model.
2. **The expert network.** The pages name twelve kinds of specialist. Confirm these people exist or are contracted before the promise is published.
3. **Success stories are patterns, not results.** The six stories show the problem, what Jwero does and what is measured, with no figures, and say so. Replace with named customers and real numbers.
4. **Photography.** The brief asks for cinematic jewellery and human photography. None exists in the repository, so the design uses type, colour and motion only. Supply images for the hero, "Get back to what you love" and the final section.
5. **The assessment** produces a plan from simple rules in the page script. It is labelled a first reading, not advice. A specialist should own the real version.
6. **Inner pages still speak the old language.** Product, solution and role pages lead with software. They were deliberately left as they are; decide whether they should be reworded to sit under the new promise.
7. **Search.** The home page title no longer leads with "jewellery software". /jwero-os keeps that title, and the product, guide and city pages still target those searches.
