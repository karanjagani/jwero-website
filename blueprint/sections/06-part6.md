## 6.1 Conversion strategy (Output #9)

### 6.1.1 The four primary funnels, end-to-end

**Funnel 1 — WhatsApp conversation (primary, sitewide).** "The conversation IS the demo."
- **Entry:** every page carries a wa.me deep link (digits-only number) with a prefilled, page-contextual message + ref code (`[ref:pricing/header]`, `[ref:pain-dead-stock/calc-result]`). The prefilled text names the page context in plain words: *"Hi, I was reading about dead stock on your site — can you show me how Jwero handles it?"* — so the prospect never faces a blank box.
- **First 60 seconds:** message lands in Jwero's own omnichannel inbox; Jwero's own AI staff answers with approval queues on (Tier A: 240+ governed actions, approval queues, daily caps, quiet hours). The prospect experiences governance and speed before any pitch. Response promise displayed at the CTA: "We reply on WhatsApp within 5 minutes, 10am–8pm — test us" — staffed as AI first-response + human follow. Publish the promise only at an SLA the team actually staffs.
- **Qualification (in-thread, conversational, max 4 questions):** business type (retail/wholesale/manufacturer) → store count → biggest current pain (chips, not typing) → city/language. AI tags the lead in Jwero CRM (90+ fields, Tier A) — dogfooding is itself the demo.
- **Handoff:** qualified → human specialist joins the same thread (no channel switch, no "someone will call you"). Enterprise-pattern answers (5+ stores, wholesale, manufacturer) route to the enterprise track (Funnel 4) inside the same conversation.
- **Follow-up automation (Jwero on Jwero):** no-reply day 2 = one contextual nudge tied to the entry page (dead-stock entry → mini ageing checklist); day 5 = case-story card `[slot — real story required, D3]`; day 12 = seasonal-readiness note; then quarterly-touch list. All caps/quiet-hours governed — the follow-up sequence itself is a screenshot-able product proof, and sales says so.

> **R:** why: fastest path from interest to product experience, uncopyable without owning the product · ICP: all, mobile-first India especially · pain: fear of demos/sales pressure · outcome: QCS/wk · objection: "is the AI real?" answered by experience · search: n/a (conversion layer) · conversion: primary CTA · KPI: WhatsApp CTA CTR, first-response time, thread→qualified rate.

**Funnel 2 — Book a demo (secondary).**
- **Entry:** /demo, plus secondary CTA on every commercial template. Calendar embed (15-min slots), 4 fields max (name, WhatsApp number, business type, store count). "Can't find a slot? WhatsApp us" beneath.
- **Pre-demo warm-up:** confirmation lands on WhatsApp (opt-in on the form), with a 3-minute story video `[slot]` and a "what to have ready" note (rough stock count, current tools) — the demo starts warmer and the prospect has pre-committed effort.
- **Demo:** persona-routed by form answers — retail demo shows counter + WhatsApp; wholesale shows catalog + B2B ordering; manufacturer shows job tracking. Never one generic demo.
- **Follow-up:** same-day recap on WhatsApp with the exact screens shown + a shareable one-pager ("show this to the family", see Part 1 — audience decision); no-show = one reschedule link + WhatsApp fallback, never a call barrage.

**Funnel 3 — Trial / pilot (tertiary; mechanics `[VERIFY-WITH-PRODUCT]`).**
- Self-serve trial activation (instant tenant, sample data, guided checklist) is **not confirmed shipped** — do not promise "start free trial in 2 minutes" until product confirms. **Fallback framing (use at launch): "Start a pilot with your own data"** — a concierge trial: we import your customer Excel + catalog, connect your WhatsApp number, and you run Assist-level features for 14 days with our team on standby.
- **Entry:** /trial (copy adapts to whichever mechanic is live), plus tertiary CTA on product pages.
- **Qualification:** pilot request form (5 fields) → WhatsApp confirmation thread (merges into Funnel 1 automation).
- **Activation definition (either mechanic):** customer file imported + WhatsApp connected + first AI-drafted message approved. That third event is the "aha" — the funnel's entire job is to reach it inside 7 days.
- **Follow-up:** day-1/3/7 WhatsApp check-ins keyed to activation steps; stalled pilots get a human call offer, not more automation.

**Funnel 4 — Enterprise enquiry (chains · wholesale · manufacturers · franchise).**
- **Entry:** /enterprise + "Talk to a specialist" CTA on chain/wholesale/manufacturer solution pages and on pricing's Chain tier. Persona-specific: MD/ops-head language, not owner-emotional copy.
- **Qualification:** short form (company, stores/units, role, current systems) OR direct specialist WhatsApp line; committee-buying support built in — downloadable evaluation kit (security overview, integration list, implementation plan template, tier-flagged capability matrix) so the champion can arm the board.
- **Handoff:** named specialist (photo + name on the thank-you state), response within 1 business day promised.
- **Follow-up:** structured sequence (evaluation kit → tailored capability mapping → pilot-branch proposal), tracked in Jwero pipeline (dogfood; Pipeline Intelligence is Tier A structure).

> **R:** why: chains/wholesale buy by committee, not impulse · ICP: multi-store, chain, wholesale, manufacturer, franchise · pain: franchise control fear, multi-branch inconsistency · outcome: enterprise pipeline · objection: "built for small shops?" · conversion: specialist calls · KPI: enterprise enquiries/mo, kit downloads, enquiry→opportunity rate.

### 6.4.2 Proof-asset collection playbook (D3: design the slots, earn the assets)
1. **Testimonials:** ask at the moment of a measurable win (first Wapsi Report with real recovered revenue), not at contract signing. Script: "Can we quote this number with your name and city?"
