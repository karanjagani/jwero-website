# PART 0 — READ ME FIRST: THE SIX DECISIONS THAT SHAPE EVERYTHING

**First principle (from the brief):** every visitor should think *"this platform understands my business better than I do."* Every page below is designed to produce that sentence in a specific visitor's head.

## 0.1 Category: the operating system for jewellery business
The founder has decided: Jwero is positioned as **the operating system for jewellery businesses** — not a CRM, not an ERP, not a messaging tool. v1's "AI Growth Engine" survives as a narrative motif (the OS's job is growth), but the category label is the OS.
- Recommended primary label: **"The AI Operating System for Jewellery Business"** — the *AI-first* qualifier is the differentiator and sidesteps SIONIQ's claim on the bare phrase "Jewelry Operating System" (see positioning research). Short forms: "the Jewellery Business OS", "Jwero OS".
- **Founder sign-off needed** on the exact label before homepage copy freezes.
- The OS claim is *earned*, not asserted: the website must show one customer record, one catalogue, one inventory truth, and modules that share state — the architecture proof pattern repeats on every product page ("this module reads and writes the same record as every other").

## 0.2 Segment-neutral by design
The platform serves every segment in the brief — 12 retail types, 6 wholesale, 6 manufacturing, 6 "others". The sitewide enemy is **disconnected software that forgets customers** — never another jeweller, never "the chains". Segment-specific comparisons live only inside that segment's /solutions page. Every sitewide claim must be true at one counter and at a hundred branches.

## 0.3 Honesty tiers are the trust moat
The website claims Tier-A product truth freely (90+ customer-record fields, 240+ governed AI actions, 14 voice languages, 5 kill-switch scopes, approval queues enforced in product), frames Tier-B as rolling out, and NEVER claims Tier-C as shipped (POS day-close, girvi, payroll, offline, vernacular product UI, SSO/public API, e-invoice, predictive ML). Tier-C items appear as transparent public-roadmap pages — the transparency itself converts sceptical, burned-before buyers. No fabricated testimonials, logos or numbers anywhere; proof slots ship empty with a collection playbook (Part 6).

## 0.4 The conversation is the demo
Primary CTA sitewide is a WhatsApp deep link with per-page ref codes, answered from Jwero's own inbox — the visitor experiences the product by using it. Secondary: Book a demo. New in v2: a **free-trial/pilot funnel** (mechanics `[VERIFY-WITH-PRODUCT]`) and a distinct **enterprise enquiry** track for chains, wholesalers and manufacturers. North star: Qualified Conversations Started/week.

## 0.5 Beautiful means the product's own design system, executed at editorial quality
Inter · white-first · royal blue #0013b7 · indigo scale · amber accent · the real flame mark · matching dark mode. "Premium/luxury/future" is achieved through layout, typography scale, photography treatment and restrained motion (Part 5) — not through gold serif clichés (v1's original theme, retired) and not through a second design language.

## 0.6 Simple words, sceptical readers
Copy must be understood by a family jeweller's patriarch, a store manager, and a first-time founder. Frameworks (PAS, StoryBrand, JTBD, Hormozi) are applied invisibly — the reader sees plain sentences about their counter, their dead stock, their WhatsApp. Hindi/Gujarati/Tamil support is answered honestly: the AI voice speaks 14 languages today; the product UI is English (roadmap for vernacular UI is public).
