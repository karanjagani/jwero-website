## What the site cannot do for itself

1. Links from trade press, associations, customers and directories (G2, Capterra, SoftwareSuggest).
2. Google Search Console and a rank tracker on the live domain.
3. A Google Business Profile and reviews.
4. Customer stories with numbers, screenshots and video.
5. Gujarati, Tamil and other language pages reviewed by a native speaker; the Hindi page should be reviewed too.
