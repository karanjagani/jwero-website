# Count your team: the numbers behind it

Date: 2026-10-05. Source: `TEAM_ROLES` in content/positioning.js and the factors
at the top of the "Count your team" block in assets/site.js.

## Status

- Employee and agency figures are drafted market averages for India (monthly,
  employee figures include the cost of employing someone). They were not taken
  from a salary survey. Check them, especially outside the metros.
- The Jwero figures are PROPOSED starting prices, set at roughly 55% of the
  average of the employee and agency figure. They are not Jwero's price list.
  Do not put this section live until the founders replace or approve them.

## Roles (employee / agency / Jwero from, rupees a month, smallest scale)

See the table in content/positioning.js; one line per role.

## Scale

| Customer base | Today's cost x | Jwero price x |
|---|---|---|
| Under 2,000 | 1 | 1 |
| 2,000 to 10,000 | 1.4 | 1.25 |
| 10,000 to 50,000 | 2 | 1.6 |
| More than 50,000 | 3 | 2.2 |

Each showroom after the first adds 12% to today's cost and 6% to the Jwero
price, up to 20 extra showrooms.

## Hours

Owner's coordination time: 1.5 hours a week per employee role, 2.5 per agency,
about 2 in total with Jwero. These are assumptions.

## Gaps

A role marked "nobody does it" adds nothing to today's cost. Its Jwero price is
shown separately and is not counted as a saving.

## The 40% guarantee

Removed from the site on 2026-10-05. The calculator shows each jeweller their
own difference instead of a blanket promise with no contract behind it.
