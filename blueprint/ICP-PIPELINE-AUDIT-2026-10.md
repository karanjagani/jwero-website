# Every ICP, scored — and the site rebuilt as one pipeline per ICP

Date: 2026-10-01. Measured on the local build (147 pages) at phone width (390px), fresh
visitor, no stored persona. 41 ICP pages measured: 23 solution/industry pages, 18 role pages.
Scores are expert judgement from the evidence in §1, out of 10. They are not results from
real jewellers. "100% confidence" cannot be promised by a website; §4 lists what would get
each ICP as close as the site can take them, and what only customers and founders can add.

---

## 1. What was measured (facts)

**Site-wide, every ICP**
- Home on a phone: 35 screens, ~2,800 words, ~14 minutes. No price. No trial link.
- Header on a phone: logo, search, theme, menu. "Get started" and "Log in" are hidden.
  Bottom bar: Chat · Call · Video demo. No trial, no price.
- The persona question ("I run a…") is 4.7 screens down the home page, not in the header.
- First visit: ~2.7 s of loader before content.
- Trial buttons land on `os.jwero.ai`, which renders a Sign-in page. Account creation is `/signup`.
- Chat widget not live (no site key): Chat opens WhatsApp, Video opens the demo form.
- Proof: 15 logos, 0 customer quotes, 0 case studies, 0 founder photos, 0 videos,
  0 real product screenshots. "Ask any of them" with no way to ask.
- Language: English only, one Hindi link.
- Referral: no jeweller-to-jeweller share, no referral offer.

**Solution pages (23)**
- Length 16–21 screens, 1,080–1,590 words. Jargon terms per page: 11–21
  (worst: jewellery-brands 21, startups 18, chains 17, B2B 17).
- All 22 segment pages have The Shift, a simulation, the day loop and the fit check.
  `/industries/retail` has none of them.
- No solution page has a trial button in its hero. Hero buttons are "Tell us about your
  business" / "Talk shop with us" + a demo or a tool.
- The price first appears 10–15 screens down, inside the "Keep reading" block.
- No customer logo appears on any solution page.
- No share button on any solution page.

**Role pages (18)**
- 12–15 screens, 900–1,260 words. Every hero says "Show me my morning in Jwero".
- No price, no day loop, no fit check on any role page. Only 7 of 18 have a simulation.
- Decision-maker roles (owner, chain owner, next-gen, franchise partner) have no trial link
  and no share button. Staff roles have "Send this page to your owner".

## 3. Gaps by ICP

**Single store** — home headline speaks to a chain ("fifty systems"); ₹1.2 lakh upfront or
₹18,000 monthly with no single-store return shown beside it; wallet cost unknowable; English.

**Product-type retail (gold, silver, diamond, gemstone, lab-grown)** — good day loops;
gold has the right simulation (rate); diamond and gemstone need a certificate-first visual;
none shows a retailer of that type as proof.

**Luxury & bridal** — promise is privacy and memory, shown only as an illustration; no
example of a private preview link; season change-freeze is stated but buried.

**Multi-store chain** — rollout plan and HQ/branch control are present; missing a case study
with a number, the security pack as a downloadable, a per-location price worked example in
the hero (the calculator is on /pricing only), and a real demo-booking calendar.

**Franchise** — same as chain, plus nothing addressed to the franchisee's own fear
(head-office control); no franchisor reference.

**Manufacturer / OEM / casting** — strongest fit (grams simulation, wastage norms); missing
the karigar's own screen, a maker's quote, and a line on what the workshop pays (same plan,
vendor/karigar logins on the wallet).

**CAD services** — honest about CAD→BOM, but the page is the thinnest fit; needs to say
plainly who this is for and who should not buy.

**Wholesale / B2B / traders / bullion** — memo exposure is described; missing a buyer-link
example, and proof from a wholesaler. Bullion: booking/rate-lock described with no visual.

**Export** — India-first rails are disclosed (good) but that makes Buy the lowest score;
needs a "what works today outside India" list rather than one caveat line.

**D2C / brands / startups** — best Try score (self-serve suits them). Startups: ₹18,000 a
month is high for a new business and the page does not address it; jargon count is highest.

**Owner / chain owner / next-gen role pages** — these are the buyers, yet their pages have
no price, no trial, no fit check and the same hero button as the cashier's page.

**Staff roles** — correct that they cannot buy; "send to your owner" exists. Missing: what
the owner receives (the brief should be the shared link), and a line that the AI drafts for
them rather than replaces them, from a real staff member.

## 4. The pipeline: ask the ICP in the header, then run one journey per ICP

Today the site is a library: 147 pages, every reader finds their own way. The change is to
make it a pipeline: one question in the header, then six stages that every ICP walks in the
same order, with the content of each stage swapped per ICP.

### 4.1 The header question

A selector in the header on every page and every screen size:
**"I run a ▾"** → Single store · Chain · Franchise · Workshop / manufacturer · Wholesale /
trade · Online brand · I work in one (staff).

- Fresh visitor: the selector is open and pulsing once on first load; the home hero shows
  the same seven choices as large buttons (replacing "Fifty systems become one" as the first
  thing asked).
- Once answered: the header shows "Single store ▾" and three stage links replace the mega
  menu on a phone — **See it · Price · Try**. The full menu stays one tap away.
- Ads, QR codes and referral links pre-answer it with `?p=`.

(The persona layer built on 2026-09-30 already stores the answer, shows a "your path" bar
and adds it to every chat message. This promotes it from a strip under the hero to the
site's main navigation.)

### 4.2 The six stages

| Stage | The visitor's question | What the page shows | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Recognise | "Is this for a business like mine?" | ICP headline, the day loop, the fit check | "Yes, this is me" → stage 2 |
| 2. See | "What does it actually look like?" | The ICP's simulation, then real screens / a 60-second recording | "Show me the price" |
| 3. Believe | "Who like me uses it?" | One named customer of that type: quote, number, photo; founders; what it doesn't do | "What does it cost me?" |
| 4. Price | "What will I pay?" | Jwero One price, the ICP's worked example (locations, registers, typical wallet), the return calculator | "Try it" or "Talk first" |
| 5. Try / talk | "Let me touch it" | Self-serve ICPs → signup. Sales-led ICPs → video demo now, or pick a time | Account or meeting |
| 6. Pass it on | "Who else should see this?" | One-tap WhatsApp share with the ICP's page and a referral offer | A second visitor, pre-answered |

A fixed progress strip (1–6) sits under the header once the ICP is known, so the visitor
always knows where they are and what the next tap is.

### 4.3 Per-ICP configuration

| ICP | Stage 1 headline | Stage 2 simulation | Stage 3 proof needed | Stage 4 price frame | Stage 5 door |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Single store | Run the whole shop from one screen | Customer memory; the till | One single-store owner, enquiries answered / customers returned | ₹9,999 vs the tools it replaces + enquiry-leakage calculator; typical wallet | Trial |
| Product-type retail | Per type (gold: "Gold moves fast…") | Rate; till; memory | A retailer of that type | Same as single store | Trial |
| Luxury & bridal | Clienteling worthy of what you sell | Memory | A boutique or bridal house, on privacy | One recovered client vs a year of plan | Video demo, then trial |
| Chain | Every branch on one record | Approval queue; HQ/branch switch | Chain case study with a number; security pack | Per-location worked example; Enterprise from six | Video demo → pilot branch |
| Franchise | Franchisor control, franchisee freedom | Approval queue | A franchisor and one franchisee | Per-location; Enterprise | Video demo → pilot location |
| Manufacturer / OEM / casting | Weighs every stage, every karigar | Metal closure | A maker's quote; the karigar screen | Plan + karigar/vendor logins; gold-loss calculator | Video demo, then trial |
| Wholesale / traders | Your inventory in every buyer's pocket | The shelf; memo mock | A wholesaler; a buyer-link example | Plan + contacts/products capacity | Trial or demo |
| Online brand / startup | Keep Shopify, add what it can't do | Memory; approval | A D2C brand with attributed revenue | Monthly option addressed head-on | Trial |
| Staff | Your day, with the AI drafting for you | Their role's simulation | A staff member's line | Not shown | Send the brief to the owner |

### 4.4 What each stage needs that does not exist yet

- **Stage 1** — move the question into the header and hero (build).
- **Stage 2** — real screenshots or a screen recording per ICP (founders).
- **Stage 3** — one quote + one number per ICP type; founder photos and video (founders).
- **Stage 4** — a per-ICP price block on each solution page, with a typical wallet figure
  (build; the wallet figure needs real usage data from founders).
- **Stage 5** — trial buttons to `/signup`; trial in the phone bar and every solution hero;
  the chat widget site key so Video opens the real product; a booking calendar (build + key).
- **Stage 6** — share button on every ICP page and after the fit check; a referral offer
  (build; the offer itself is a founder decision).

### 4.5 Fix first (highest effect, no founder input needed)

1. Header ICP selector + hero ICP buttons; stage links on phones.
2. Trial button to `/signup`, in the phone bar and in every solution-page hero.
3. Price block per ICP on every solution page and on the owner role pages.
4. Share button on every solution page; the brief as the staff share target.
5. Owner / chain-owner / next-gen role pages: add price, trial or demo, fit check.
6. `/industries/retail`: add the playbook blocks or redirect to the retail segment pages.
7. Jargon pass on the six highest-count pages; shorten the loader on phones.

### 4.6 Needs founders (in order of effect on Trust and Buy)

1. One quote and one number per ICP type from the fifteen customers.
2. Real screens: the counter, the inbox, a customer record, the karigar view.
3. Founder photos and a short video.
4. A typical monthly wallet figure per ICP; whether a lower entry option exists for startups.
5. The referral offer.
6. The chat widget site key.
7. Hindi and Gujarati for the retail pipeline.
