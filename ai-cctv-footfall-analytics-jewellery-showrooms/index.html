    Check your cameras

  2. 2

QUESTIONS

What people ask before they message.

What is AI CCTV footfall analytics?

Using AI on your existing CCTV footage to count the people who enter and leave, and how full the floor is, then comparing it with sales. Jwero does this for jewellery showrooms and matches footfall to bills.

Can I use my existing CCTV cameras?

Yes. IP cameras and NVRs that stream over RTSP work, including Hikvision, Dahua and CP Plus, and ONVIF cameras. Nothing new to buy for counting.

Do I need a separate people counter?

No. The AI counts from the camera already pointing at your door, through a small on-site connector.

Does it recognise faces?

No. It counts people; it does not recognise faces, identify customers or tell a repeat visitor from a new one. Customers are identified only when staff check them in.

How is footfall turned into conversion?

Footfall is matched to the bills raised in the same hours, so you see conversion by hour, day, branch and salesperson.

What if a camera goes offline?

You get an alert, and gaps in counting are flagged so a bad day is not mistaken for a quiet one.

Is CCTV footfall counting legal?

Showrooms may use cameras, but visitors should be told and personal data handled under the data protection law. Jwero has retention, signage and capture settings; take advice for your own case.

How is it charged?

Camera counting is charged per camera. See pricing.
