PRICING QUESTIONS

Straight answers on cost and terms.

How much does Jwero cost?

There are three ways to buy. Run it yourself: ₹18,000 a month with every module, first month ₹3,600, extra locations ₹2,999 each. Let Jwero run it: no subscription, every tool included, priced on the work, about half of what that work costs you today. Enterprise for groups and chains: custom. Messages, AI and calls are charged from a prepaid wallet at published rates.

What does Jwero cost?

One plan, Jwero One: ₹18,000 a month, billed monthly. Every module is included. Prices exclude GST. Groups and chains take Enterprise, which is custom priced.

Is there a free trial?

No. Instead, your first month of Jwero One is ₹3,600 instead of ₹18,000, with every module. Per-use services such as WhatsApp messages and AI run on a prepaid wallet from day one. The first-month price applies once per business.

What is the wallet?

The plan covers the whole platform. Things that cost money each time they happen, a WhatsApp marketing message, an AI image, a call minute, a payout, are metered from a prepaid wallet at the published rates on this page. You top it up; nothing is charged to a card behind your back.

Do I pay per module, or per user?

Neither. Jwero One includes every module, and there is no per-seat price for your team. You turn off the modules you do not use so the screen stays simple. What scales with you is capacity: extra locations, brands, registers and storage, at the rates shown.

I run more than one store. What does that cost?

One location is included. Each additional location is ₹2,999 a month. Use the calculator above for your count; chains that want negotiated terms, unlimited history or dedicated support should ask for Enterprise.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Billing is month to month and you can cancel any time. Your data exports whenever you ask.

How long is my history kept?

On Jwero One: three years of audit and analytics history, and message history kept without a limit. Enterprise keeps everything without a limit. Your books, orders, invoices, payments, purchases, payroll and GST records, are statutory and are never deleted on any plan.

Are there hidden costs?

The plan price and the rate card are both on this page. Onboarding help is priced per session if you want it; importing your data and connecting your number are part of getting started.

How should I think about the cost?

Against the tools it replaces and the customers it recovers, a WhatsApp tool, a catalogue app, a website subscription, an SMS vendor and the hours spent reconciling them. Run the calculators on your own numbers before you decide.

Why should I believe the return?

You should not take our word for it, that is what the growth report is for. It is an account of what happened with your own customers, on your own data, inside your first month.

“Another software cost?”

Measure it against the tools it replaces and one recovered customer, not against your billing software’s AMC. Run the calculators on your own numbers. Open the calculators.

“I don’t need the whole platform?”

You are not charged per module, so there is nothing to trim. Switch off what you don’t use and the screen shows only what you run.

“Hidden costs will show up later?”

The plan price and the wallet rate card are both on this page, and the same card is inside your billing screen.

“I’ll be locked into a contract?”

Billing is month to month. Cancel any time, and export everything when you leave.

“What will WhatsApp and AI add?”

They run on a prepaid wallet at per-use rates. You see the balance and the spend; nothing is charged beyond what you top up.

“ROI is a promise I’ve heard before?”

Fair. That is why the first month is ₹3,600, on your own data, and why the growth report shows what happened with your own customers.

MANAGED PRICING

How the managed price is worked out.

When Jwero runs the work, there is no subscription and every tool is included. The price follows the work.

  1. 01Your volume

    Conversations, calls, posts and orders, predicted from your customer base and showrooms, or counted from your own figures.

  2. 02Today’s cost

    What that work costs at the cheapest way to staff it in India: a junior hire or a freelancer.

  3. 03Jwero’s price

    About half of that when you let Jwero decide and execute, and about 60% when you want to approve every step.

  4. 04In writing

    Your plan states the price, the work and what is measured before anything starts.
