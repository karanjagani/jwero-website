WHATSAPP QUESTIONS

Bans, rule changes, and what sets this apart.

Will my number get banned?

No. Jwero uses the official WhatsApp Business API with approved templates, consent tracking and opt-out handling, the discipline that keeps accounts healthy.

What if Meta changes the rules?

Your customer records and catalogue live in Jwero, not inside the channel. Channels can change; your data doesn’t move with them.

How is this different from WATI or similar tools?

They send messages. Jwero replies come from a system that knows the customer and the jewellery, because they share one record. See Jwero vs WATI.
