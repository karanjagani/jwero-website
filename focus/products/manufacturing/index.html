FROM DESIGN TO FINISHED PIECE

One production order, weighed at every hand-off.

  1. 1

    Bill of materials

    Metal, stones, findings and their expected weights per design, the composition every later step is checked against.

  2. 2

    Routing & release

    Casting, filing, setting, polishing, hallmarking: the routing resolves from the design’s category and metal, and release explodes BOM and routing in one step.

  3. 3

    Issue desk

    Metal and stones issued to a karigar or a stage, pre-filled from the BOM and the sales order, weighed on the way out.

  4. 4

    Wastage norms

    Each stage has a norm. Loss beyond it is flagged; loss within it is booked. Nothing disappears into “process loss”.

  5. 5

    QC & hallmarking

    Inspection with dispositions, pass, rework, scrap, and HUID/hallmark recorded on the piece before it can be sold.

  6. 6

    Finished-goods receipt

    The finished piece is minted into inventory with its identity, its lots and its cost, ready for the catalogue and the counter.

THE KARIGAR SIDE

A khata that closes, not a notebook that argues.

Karigar wages in jewellery are settled against metal, not hours. Jwero keeps that ledger the way the trade keeps it, and posts it to the books.

Karigar directory & open jobs

Who is holding what, since when, and when it is due, swept daily for jobs running late.

Metal reconciliation

Issued, returned, wastage within norm, wastage beyond norm, per karigar, per job, per period.

Settlement under your policy

Making charges and wastage allowances by policy; settlement converts finished work to a payable and posts it to the ledger in the same step.

Karigar self-service

My Work, Khata, Loans and Assets on the karigar’s own phone, with an early Hindi pilot on these screens.

Scorecards

Turnaround, rework rate and loss per karigar, so the next job goes to the right bench.

Job cards & documents

Printable job cards, BOM sheets and finished-goods receipts, generated from the order, not retyped.

BECAUSE IT'S ONE SYSTEM

  • A custom order taken on WhatsApp becomes the production order, the customer’s size, stone and deadline carry through without re-entry.
  • The finished piece is the same record the catalogue publishes, the storefront sells and the counter scans.
  • Karigar settlement, stone loss and work-in-progress post to the same ledger as sales, the workshop is inside the books, not beside them.

WORKSHOP QUESTIONS

Loss, karigars and starting from a paper register.

We have years of karigar khatas on paper. Can we start?

Yes, opening balances per karigar are entered at onboarding; the ledger runs forward from there.

Can a job go through an outside workshop?

Yes, job-work issues and receipts are tracked the same way as internal stages, with the party’s ledger alongside.

Does it work for diamond and gemstone manufacturing too?

Stone lots, setting stages and sorting loss are modelled; the gold flow and the stone flow reconcile on the same order.
