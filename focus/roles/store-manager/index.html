HOW TO CONTRIBUTE MORE

Concrete ways to add value using what Jwero already gives you.

  1. 1

    Start each day with the morning report

    Use the plain-language report to walk the floor with a plan instead of finding out what happened as it happens.

  2. 2

    Review the approval queue as a coaching tool

    When you edit a drafted reply, that’s a moment to show a staff member what "good" looks like, not just a task to clear.

  3. 3

    Use ageing and dead-stock visibility to steer buying conversations

    Bring the fast/slow-mover data to purchase discussions instead of relying on memory of what "usually sells".

STORE MANAGER QUESTIONS

Straight answers about the role and the change.

Does this replace my job, or just my paperwork?

Just the paperwork and the guesswork. AI staff draft follow-ups, reminders and reports; you and your team approve what goes out. Deciding how to run the floor, who to coach, and which customer gets a call from you personally, that stays entirely yours.

Can I see what every branch or counter is doing without calling around?

Yes, stock, sales and staff activity across branches roll up into one view, with per-branch exceptions routed through approvals so you keep control without needing to phone each store.

How much of my day does this actually save?

It depends on your store, but the pattern is consistent: less time spent re-asking staff "what happened today" or chasing enquiries that went cold, more time spent on the floor with customers and on coaching your team.

Do I need to be technical to use this?

No, the daily view is built around a plain-language report and an approval queue, not a dense dashboard. If you can use WhatsApp, you can run the store view.
