Modern jewellery customers expect convenience.

They want to explore designs, compare options, and shortlist products without visiting a store immediately.

WhatsApp has become one of the most effective channels for making this possible.

Instead of sending dozens of individual product images, jewellery retailers can use WhatsApp catalogues to create a professional shopping experience directly inside chat.

A well-organised catalogue helps customers discover products faster and improves the overall buying experience.

What Is a WhatsApp Catalogue?

A WhatsApp catalogue is a digital product showcase available inside WhatsApp Business and WhatsApp Business API.

Businesses can display:

  • Product images
  • Product names
  • Pricing
  • Descriptions
  • Product categories

Customers can browse products without leaving the conversation.

Why Catalogue Sharing Matters for Jewellers

Jewellery purchases are highly visual.

Customers want to:

  • View designs
  • Compare products
  • Explore collections
  • Share options with family

Catalogues simplify this process.

Instead of searching through multiple messages, customers can browse a structured collection.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a WhatsApp catalogue?

A WhatsApp catalogue is a digital product showcase that allows businesses to display products directly inside WhatsApp conversations.

Why should jewellers use WhatsApp catalogues?

Catalogues make product discovery easier, improve customer experience, and help customers explore collections more efficiently.

Can WhatsApp catalogues increase jewellery sales?

Yes. Better product visibility and easier browsing often lead to higher engagement and conversions.

What products should be included in a jewellery catalogue?

Popular categories include bridal jewellery, diamond collections, gold jewellery, earrings, necklaces, and festival collections.

Can catalogues be personalised?

Yes. Different customer segments can receive different product recommendations and collections.

How does CRM improve catalogue sharing?

CRM helps businesses understand customer interests, track engagement, and recommend relevant products.
