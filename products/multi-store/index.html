ADDING A BRANCH

How to add a new branch to your jewellery chain.

Five steps.

  1. 1

    Set up the branch

    Name, address, GSTIN if in another state, counters and cash registers.

  2. 2

    Give people their roles

    Branch manager, cashiers and sales staff, each seeing only what they should.

  3. 3

    Load or transfer its stock

    Opening stock imported, or pieces transferred in from other branches on challans.

  4. 4

    Apply the network’s rules

    Central prices, catalogue, schemes and campaigns, with any branch exceptions approved.

  5. 5

    Open the doors

    The branch bills, takes scheme payments and gets its WhatsApp chats from day one.

QUESTIONS

What people ask before they message.

What does Jwero’s multi-store jewellery software for chains & franchises do?

Multi-store jewellery software: one customer, one stock and central prices across branches, schemes redeemable anywhere, one WhatsApp number routed to the right branch, branch reports and roles. It is part of Jwero One: ₹18,000 a month, every module included, with the first month at ₹3,600.

What is multi-store jewellery software?

Software that runs a chain of jewellery showrooms as one business: one customer record, one stock you can see and transfer across branches, central prices and campaigns, branch-by-branch reports, and roles so each branch sees what it should.

How do jewellery chains control prices across branches?

Set price rules centrally, from the rate, making and stones, and let branches request exceptions that go to a manager for approval. Every branch then quotes the same piece the same way.

Can a customer redeem a scheme at any branch?

Yes. A member can enrol at one branch, pay instalments at another and redeem at any branch in the network, on the same scheme record.

Can one WhatsApp number serve the whole chain?

Yes. Customers message one number and each chat is routed to the right branch, with the customer’s history visible to whoever answers.

How are stock transfers between branches handled for GST?

Within one state, transfers move on a delivery challan. Between states, branches have different GSTINs and the transfer is billed with GST. Confirm your setup with your CA.

Can franchise partners see only their own store?

Yes. Roles and permissions are set at each level, so a franchise partner sees their store while head office sees the network. Franchise royalty calculation is not built yet.

Will branch managers resist losing autonomy?

Branches keep running their day. Head office sets the rules that customers expect to be the same everywhere, and branches request exceptions through approvals.

Does a customer’s history follow them between branches?

Yes. Purchases, schemes, loyalty points and conversations are on one record, visible at every counter.

Can each branch keep billing if the internet drops?

Yes. Each counter keeps billing offline, and sales sync when the connection returns.
