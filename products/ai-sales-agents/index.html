THE STAFF QUESTION

What your salespeople should worry about.

What does Jwero’s AI for jewellers do?

AI sales agents that answer, follow up, and call customers back in 14 languages, on WhatsApp, web chat, and phone/IVR via Exotel or Tata Tele, governed by approval queues, daily caps and kill switches. It is part of Jwero One: ₹18,000 a month, every module included, with the first month at ₹3,600.

Will this replace my sales staff?

No. The AI workforce does the remembering and follow-up; your people do the selling. Salespeople close more when every customer walks in already known.

My salespeople are worried about being watched or replaced. What do I tell them?

It works for them, not on them, it does the tedious remembering so they spend their time on the sale itself.

Can it give a discount without me knowing?

No, pricing and discounts follow your price rules and staff permissions, always.

Can customers video-call or watch a live stream to see a piece?

Yes, live streams, shoppable video and video-QR all connect customers with your team on camera. These are live-video capabilities, not an AI-hosted stream; only the surrounding text is AI-drafted, and always under approval.

More questions? See every AI trust question →

More questions jewellers ask.

Can AI call my customers for scheme reminders?

Yes. The AI voice agent places reminder and follow-up calls in the customer’s language over a telephony line you connect, and writes the outcome on her record. Calls are charged per minute.

What is an AI sales agent in Jwero?

A configured member of the AI workforce: a scope of allowed actions, a knowledge base, guardrails, an approval workflow and an activity log. It drafts replies, follows up, reminds and invites, within the limits you set.

Can the AI really speak on calls?

Yes, in two ways. On WhatsApp and your website’s web chat, AI voice is native to Jwero, no third party involved, and converses in 14 languages, with transcripts filed on the customer record. For actual phone calls (instalment reminders, outbound follow-ups, IVR), the AI agent speaks over a telephony line you connect, currently in 11 Indian languages.

Does Jwero do IVR?

Yes, through your telephony provider, Jwero’s AI voice agent runs outbound and inbound phone calls, including IVR menus, over a connected line from Exotel, Tata Tele, or most other telephony/CPaaS providers on request. That’s different from voice on WhatsApp and web chat, which is fully native to Jwero and needs no telephony connection at all. See Integrations for the telephony connectors.

How do I know what the AI did?

Every action is logged: what, when, why, and who approved it. You can review any day’s activity in minutes.
