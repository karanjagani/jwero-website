More questions jewellers ask.

What does Jwero’s AI for jewellers do?

AI chatbot and voice AI for jewellers: answers on WhatsApp, Instagram and web chat with pieces at today’s rate, follows up and calls back in 14 languages, 24/7, under your approvals, daily caps and kill switch. It is part of Jwero One: ₹18,000 a month, every module included, with the first month at ₹3,600.

Can AI call my customers for scheme reminders?

Yes. The AI voice agent places reminder and follow-up calls in the customer’s language over a telephony line you connect, and writes the outcome on her record. Calls are charged per minute.

What is an AI chatbot for jewellers?

An assistant that answers customers on WhatsApp, Instagram and your website with real pieces at today’s rate, follows up, and books visits. Jwero’s also speaks and calls, in 14 languages, under your approval.

What is an AI sales agent in Jwero?

A member of the AI workforce with a set of allowed actions, a knowledge base, limits, an approval workflow and an activity log. It drafts replies, follow-ups, reminders and invitations within the limits you set.

Does it speak Hindi and other languages?

Yes. Chat, voice and phone calls run in 14 languages, including Arabic, Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and English.

Can the AI make phone calls?

Yes. AI voice is built into WhatsApp and web chat, and phone calls and IVR run over your telephony provider, with transcripts on the customer record.

Does it share products and prices?

Yes. Replies include pieces from your catalogue at today’s rate, chosen from her taste and past purchases.

Will this replace my sales staff?

No. The AI does the remembering and the follow-up; your people do the selling, and every customer walks in already known.

Can it give a discount without me knowing?

No. Prices and discounts follow your price rules and staff permissions. The AI drafts messages; it does not set prices.

How do I know what the AI did?

Every action is logged: what, when, why, and who approved it.

Which AI agents come ready?

Twelve agent teams: Revenue, Sales, Seller, Marketing, Growth, CX, Operations, Inventory, Finance, Reporting, HR, and Payroll and Settlement. Most ask for approval by default.

Can staff talk to Jwero instead of clicking?

Yes. An in-app assistant takes spoken or typed instructions, including Hindi and Hinglish phrases, and a staff voice assistant wakes on a wake word.

Does AI read call transcripts?

Yes. After a call, AI reads the transcript, notes how interested the customer was, and updates her scores.
