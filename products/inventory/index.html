EVERY DOOR

Every way a piece leaves the vault, and comes back.

Jewellery stock rarely just sits or sells. It goes out on memo, to an exhibition, on approval to a good customer, to a karigar, to another branch. Each door is a ledger entry, not a sticky note.

Memo & approval

Pieces out on approval memo to a customer or the trade, with due dates and a return-or-bill close.

Consignment

Stock placed with a partner store or taken from a supplier, valued and reconciled as its own ledger.

Exhibitions

A show’s stock goes out as a set, sells or returns piece by piece, and reconciles when the stand closes.

Trials & reservations

Held for a customer with an expiry, released automatically if she does not come back.

Vaults & transfers

Vault to showcase, branch to branch, with a documented trail for every high-value transit.

Counts & discrepancies

Scheduled or spot counts; every mismatch becomes a discrepancy to resolve, not a shrug.

Hallmarking

HUID and hallmark status carried on the piece; unhallmarked stock is flagged before it reaches the counter.

Labels & item ledger

Print tags from the record; every movement of a piece, from receipt to sale, on one ledger line.

INVENTORY QUESTIONS

Stocktakes, and starting from messy records.

Will this replace our physical stocktake?

It makes stocktakes faster and less of a surprise-finding exercise, ageing and valuation are visible continuously.

Our stock records are inconsistent across branches. Can you still start?

Yes, we import what exists per branch and reconcile during onboarding. Normal starting point, not disqualifying.

More questions? See the full FAQ →

More questions jewellers ask.

Can I manage jewellery stock in Excel or Google Sheets?

You can list it, but a sheet does not change when a piece is sold, cannot value stock at today’s rate, and has no record of who edited it. It works for a few hundred pieces and breaks beyond that.

Can Jwero tell me my dead stock?

Yes. Ageing bands (0–30, 31–90, 91–180, 180+ days) and fast/slow-mover views show exactly which pieces are sitting, for how long, and what they are worth at today’s rate.

Does it work across branches?

Yes, stock, transfers and valuation are branch-aware, with the full picture rolled up for the owner.

Can it forecast demand?

Not yet, predictive demand forecasting is on the roadmap, and we will say so until it ships. Today Jwero gives you valuation, ageing and mover analysis: the visibility layer that shows what to buy next.
