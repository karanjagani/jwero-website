QUESTIONS

What people ask before they message.

What does Jwero’s jewellery quotation and estimate software do?

Jewellery quotation and estimate software: numbered quotes priced at today’s gold rate with your making and wastage rules, sent on WhatsApp or email, accepted from her phone, followed up and converted to an order. It is part of Jwero One: ₹18,000 a month, every module included, with the first month at ₹3,600.

What is a jewellery estimate?

A written price for chosen pieces before the sale: metal at today’s rate, making charges and stones. In Jwero it is a numbered quotation the customer can accept from her phone.

How do I make a jewellery estimate?

Pick the pieces from your catalogue; Jwero prices them at today’s rate with your making and wastage rules, and sends a numbered link and PDF on WhatsApp or email.

What is the difference between a quotation, an estimate and an invoice?

An estimate or quotation is the price offered before she decides. An invoice is the bill after the sale. In Jwero an accepted quotation converts to an order, and the invoice follows.

How does a quotation get created?

From her record, from a catalogue enquiry in one step, or by an automation. Sending always needs your confirm.

Can the customer accept it online?

Yes. She opens the link, sees the numbered quotation with its lines and PDF, and accepts or declines. It is written to her record.

Is the rate locked?

Every quotation has a validity window. Whether the rate is held inside it is your rule.

Does an accepted quote become an order?

Yes. It converts to a sales order with the quoted lines.

Can a bridal family see one quotation?

Yes. Everyone opens the same numbered document, so there is no argument about which price was final.
