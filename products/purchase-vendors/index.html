QUESTIONS

What people ask before they message.

What does Jwero’s jewellery purchase & vendor management software do?

Jewellery purchase and vendor management software: AI-drafted orders, unfixed-rate gold purchases, receiving with quality checks, bill matching, GSTR-2B (India) reconciliation, metal loans and a vendor portal. It is part of Jwero One, the single plan with every module included. You start with a free trial, and your price is shown in your account when it ends.

What is jewellery purchase management software?

Software for buying jewellery, gold and stones: purchase orders with per-vendor prices, receiving by weight and purity with quality checks, bills matched to the order and receipt, returns, vendor advances and payments, GST input credit checked against GSTR-2B, and metal purchases in fine grams.

Can AI draft purchase orders?

Yes. Jwero drafts purchase orders from what is selling fast, what is running low and what is ageing, with each vendor’s prices. Your team reviews and sends them.

Can I buy gold before the rate is fixed?

Yes. Record an unfixed-rate purchase by weight and purity, and fix the rate later, as is common with bullion dealers and suppliers. The bill and the metal ledger update when the rate is fixed.

How do jewellers reconcile purchases with GSTR-2B?

Match every purchase bill against GSTR-2B each month. Bills a supplier has not filed show up as gaps, so you can chase them before claiming input credit. Jwero does this matching for you.

Should gold purchases be recorded in fine grams or rupees?

Both. Rupees for the accounts, and fine grams for the metal ledger, so metal bought, issued, sold and owed can be compared whatever the rate.

What is a vendor portal?

A separate login where each supplier sees their orders, delivery status, bills and payments, so they stop calling to ask. Nothing to install.

How do jewellers handle consignment stock from suppliers?

Record it as stock you hold but do not own, sell from it, and settle with the supplier for what sold. Jwero keeps consignment stock separate and produces the settlement.

Can I set different prices per vendor?

Yes. Each vendor’s prices and terms are kept on their record and used on their orders.

Is this the same as manufacturing job work?

No. Purchase is buying from suppliers. Sending metal to karigars for making is job work, handled in manufacturing on challans.
