More questions jewellers ask.

What is Jwero Ecommerce?

Jwero Ecommerce is a jewellery website that sells like your showroom: prices at today’s gold rate with the breakup, purity, hallmark ID and certificates on every piece, the same stock as your counter, reserve and collect at a branch, showroom visits and gold plans online. Optimize is built in: heatmaps, A/B tests, funnels, personalisation by audience, popups, web push, webchat and journeys for carts left behind, with every visitor on one customer record.

How do I build a jewellery website with live gold rates?

Use a website where each piece is priced from weight, purity and the day’s rate, so prices change when the rate does. Jwero’s ecommerce website does this from the same catalogue and stock as your counter.

How do I list jewellery on Google Shopping?

Google needs a product feed with price, image and availability. Jwero keeps that product data accurate in one catalogue, so the feed you give Google carries the same prices and stock as your counter.

What is the best ecommerce platform for jewellers in India?

The best jewellery ecommerce platform follows the gold rate, shows the price breakup, proves purity with hallmark ID and certificates, and sells from the same stock as the showroom. Jwero Ecommerce does all four, with reserve-and-collect, gold plans online and website optimisation built in.

How do I start an online jewellery store?

Bring your catalogue with weights, purity and making charges, choose a jewellery theme, connect your domain, switch on payments, reserve-and-collect and gold plans, then go live. Jwero Ecommerce uses the same catalogue as your counter and AI to set up the pages.

How do jewellery websites show live gold rate prices?

Each product is priced from today’s rate by purity and net weight, plus making charges and stones. Jwero Ecommerce shows this breakup line by line and checks the price again at checkout so nobody pays yesterday’s rate.

Should a jewellery website show the price breakup?

Yes. Shoppers trust a price they can check: metal at today’s rate, making charges, stones and tax. Jwero Ecommerce shows the breakup on every product page.

How do I build trust on a jewellery website?

Show purity, weights, hallmark ID and certificates on every piece, real reviews with photos and answered questions. Jwero Ecommerce puts all of these on the product page.

Can customers reserve jewellery online and collect it in store?

Yes. In Jwero Ecommerce a customer reserves a piece at the branch that holds it and collects it with a pickup code, or books a showroom visit in a time slot.

Can customers pay a gold savings plan online?

Yes. Jwero Ecommerce lets customers see plans with a calculator, enrol, pay each instalment, set up UPI autopay and follow their passbook from their account.

Which payment options work on a jewellery website?

Jwero Ecommerce supports Razorpay, Cashfree, PhonePe and UPI, shows EMI options on the product page where you offer them, and applies coupons and loyalty points at checkout.

How do I increase conversion on a jewellery website?

Find where visitors drop, test the fix and keep the winner. Jwero Ecommerce includes funnels, heatmaps, A/B tests, personalisation, popups and web push, with every visitor on one customer record.

Can a jewellery website show different content to different visitors?

Yes. Jwero Ecommerce shows banners and product-page blocks by audience, and recommends pieces from each visitor’s own browsing and purchases.

How do jewellers recover abandoned carts?

Follow up while the shopper still cares. In Jwero Ecommerce, an abandoned cart triggers the WhatsApp or email journey you set up, and her cart stays on her record for your team.

Can customers search jewellery by photo?

Yes. Jwero Ecommerce lets a shopper upload a photo and see the closest pieces in your catalogue.

Will a jewellery website help me rank on Google?

Jwero Ecommerce gives you what ranking needs: fast pages checked with Google PageSpeed, structured data, a sitemap, clean addresses and redirects, a blog, collection pages and reviews.

Do customers need a password to buy?

No. Jwero Ecommerce offers guest checkout and sign-in with a one-time code on WhatsApp, SMS or email.

Is the online store connected to my showroom stock?

Yes. Jwero Ecommerce sells from the same stock as your counter, so a piece is never sold twice.

How much does a jewellery ecommerce website cost?

Jwero Ecommerce starts with a free trial that includes every module; your price is shown inside your account after the trial.
