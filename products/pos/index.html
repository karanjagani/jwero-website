More questions jewellers ask.

What does Jwero’s jewellery billing software & POS for jewellery shops do?

Jewellery billing software and POS: price at today’s gold rate, scan tags, old gold exchange, HUID check, estimates, split payments, WhatsApp receipts and cash day-close. It is part of Jwero One: ₹18,000 a month, every module included, with the first month at ₹3,600.

Which POS is best for a jewellery showroom?

One that prices by weight at the live rate, handles old-gold exchange, returns and scheme redemption, and closes the cash by register. A general retail POS does none of these.

What is jewellery billing software?

Jewellery billing software prices and bills pieces the way a jewellery counter works: by net weight and purity at today’s gold rate, plus making, stones and 3% GST, with old gold exchange, estimates, HUID checks, returns and the cash day-close.

Which is the best billing software for a jewellery shop?

Look for automatic pricing at today’s rate, tag scanning, old gold exchange on the same bill, a HUID check, estimates, returns, cash day-close, billing that keeps working offline, and the customer record behind every bill. Jwero does all of these on one system.

How is the gold price calculated on a bill?

Metal value is today’s 24K rate × karat ÷ 24 × net weight. Add making, stones and other charges, then 3% GST. Jwero does this on every bill automatically; try the calculator on this page.

Does it handle HUID and old gold?

Yes. The counter checks each piece’s HUID and can warn or block an unhallmarked sale. Old gold is taken in on an exchange voucher with weight, purity and stone deduction, and applied as credit on the new bill.

What is the difference between jewellery POS and billing software?

They are the same job at the counter. Jewellery POS usually means the whole till: scanning, pricing, payments, returns and the cash day-close. Jewellery billing software is how many jewellers search for it. Jwero does both on one screen.

Can I use it without internet?

Yes. The counter installs on the till device and keeps billing through a dropout; each sale syncs once the connection returns, without duplicates.

Can a customer pay with UPI, card and cash on one bill?

Yes. The cashier records cash, card, UPI and credit, split across one bill. Card machines are not driven by Jwero; the cashier records the amount taken on the machine.

Can I give an estimate and turn it into a bill later?

Yes. Estimates are saved on the customer’s record and become a bill in one step, repriced at that day’s rate.

Do I still need my old billing software?

Not for the sale, the return or the till. Your books can stay in Tally or Zoho Books through the bridge, or run on Jwero’s own ledger.

Can two cashiers share a register?

A register has one open shift at a time, so takings are always attributable to the person who opened it.

Can a salesperson be credited for the sale?

Yes. The salesperson on the bill feeds incentives and the sales reports.
