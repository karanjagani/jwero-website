SWITCHING ERP MID-YEAR

How to switch your jewellery ERP mid-year.

Five steps, done with you. No big-bang cut-over.

  1. 1

    Bring your masters

    Stock, customers, vendors, karigars and opening balances from your current ERP or Tally.

  2. 2

    Map your way of working

    Rates, making rules, branches, approval rules and who can do what.

  3. 3

    Run both in parallel

    Bill and record in Jwero while the old system runs, until daily totals match.

  4. 4

    Reconcile the cut-over

    Stock, metal and money balances matched on the switch date, differences listed and cleared.

  5. 5

    Switch off the old system

    Your books carry on in Jwero’s ledger or through the Tally bridge.

QUESTIONS

What people ask before they message.

What does Jwero’s jewellery ERP software do?

Jewellery ERP software: counter, stock, purchase, material planning, manufacturing, girvi, accounts, GST, payroll and branches on one record, with WhatsApp and customers built in. It is part of Jwero One: ₹18,000 a month, every module included, with the first month at ₹3,600.

What is jewellery ERP software?

Jewellery ERP software runs the whole jewellery business on one system: counter billing at today’s rate, stock by piece and fine weight, purchase, material planning, manufacturing and karigar job work, girvi, accounts and GST, payroll and every branch, on the same records.

What is the best ERP for jewellers?

One that is built for jewellery (weight, purity, fine metal, HUID, making and wastage), covers every department on one record, works across branches and devices, keeps billing offline, and connects to your customers on WhatsApp. Compare a few on your own data before you choose.

Can a jewellery ERP replace Tally?

Jwero keeps its own double-entry ledger with GST, so many shops run their books in Jwero. Others keep Tally for their CA and send entries through the bridge; e-invoices are generated in Tally that way.

Does Jwero handle manufacturing and material planning?

Yes. Bills of materials, routings, material planning, work in progress, karigar job work with wastage norms, finished-goods receipts, and a fine-weight metal ledger with metal loans.

Does it handle girvi?

Yes. Pledges, interest, renewals, part payments, release and auction notices are part of Jwero, on the same customer record.

How do I switch ERP mid-year?

Import your masters and opening balances, run Jwero alongside the old system until totals match, reconcile stock, metal and money on the switch date, then switch off the old system. We do it with you.

Will switching disrupt operations mid-order?

No. Open orders, job work and balances are brought across, and both systems run in parallel until you are ready.

Our process is unusual. Can it be configured?

Yes. Approval rules, roles, branches, making and wastage rules and custom fields are set to how you work.

Does Jwero generate e-invoices?

E-invoices are generated in Tally: Jwero raises the bill and sends it through the Tally bridge. GST reports such as GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and the HSN summary are prepared in Jwero.
