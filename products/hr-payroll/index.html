More questions jewellers ask.

What is Jwero HR & Payroll?

Jwero HR & Payroll runs a jewellery business’s people work: attendance from kiosks, phones or biometric exports, shifts and leave, pay runs with PF, ESI, PT and TDS, sales incentives from real sales with clawback, karigar payments kept separate, loans and expenses, hiring to full and final, appraisals and training, with every entry posted to the books.

How do I hire sales staff for a jewellery showroom?

Track each candidate through applied, screening, interview, offer and hired in one pipeline. A hired candidate moves straight into onboarding with a checklist, so documents and training are not forgotten.

What is the best HR and payroll software for a jewellery shop?

The best HR and payroll software for a jewellery shop counts attendance, pays sales incentives from real bills with clawback on returns, runs PF, ESI, PT and TDS, and keeps karigar payments apart. Jwero HR & Payroll does all of this on the same record as your sales and ledger.

How do I calculate a salesperson’s incentive in a jewellery shop?

In Jwero each bill is credited to the salesperson who made it, and the incentive is worked out on your slabs, on sales or on margin. For example, ₹18 lakh of sales at 1% earns ₹18,000; a returned ₹1.2 lakh sale claws back ₹1,200, so ₹16,800 is paid with salary.

Is incentive clawed back when a jewellery sale is returned?

Yes. In Jwero, when a sale is returned the incentive earned on it is clawed back from the salesperson in the next pay run, so you never pay incentive on jewellery that came back.

How do jewellery shops track staff attendance?

Jewellery shops on Jwero track attendance with a kiosk at the counter, phone punches checked by geofence and selfie, or punches imported from a biometric device. Late marks, overtime and leave are then worked out on their own.

Does Jwero work with ESSL or ZKTeco biometric attendance devices?

Jwero imports punches from the exports of biometric devices such as ESSL and ZKTeco, matches them to each person, and counts late marks, overtime and leave. It does not keep a live connection to the device.

How can I track staff attendance across jewellery branches?

Jwero shows who is in, late and on leave at every branch on one screen. Staff punch at a kiosk or on their phone with geofence and selfie, so a punch only counts at the right branch.

Can jewellery payroll software handle PF, ESI, PT and TDS?

Jwero payroll works out PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS, with tax declarations, in each monthly pay run, and prepares the statutory exports for your accountant to file.

Does Jwero pay salaries into bank accounts?

Jwero prepares a bank file from the approved pay run, which you upload to your bank to pay salaries. Nothing is paid before the run is approved by the person you choose.

How are karigars paid in jewellery payroll software?

In Jwero karigar payments sit on their own ledger, apart from staff salaries. Jobs are settled, TDS is worked out, and a separate karigar bank file is prepared, with entries posted to the books.

Do jewellery staff need an app for attendance and payslips?

No app is needed. Jwero gives each person a staff area on the web where they punch in with geofence and selfie, see their leave balance, payslips and incentive so far, and send leave requests.

How are advances and loans to jewellery staff recovered?

Jwero records each advance or loan and deducts the instalments in payroll automatically, so advances given at the counter are never forgotten at month end.

How is Diwali overtime and festival duty handled in payroll?

Jwero rosters extra shifts for festival weeks, works out overtime from punches, and gives comp-off for festival duty, all counted in the same month’s pay run.

Is payroll software better than Excel for a jewellery shop?

Excel needs the attendance, incentive and deduction sheets updated by hand every month. Jwero works them out from punches and bills, runs one approved pay run, and posts the entries to the ledger.

How does a jewellery shop move payroll from Excel to software?

With Jwero you export staff, salary structures and balances, set your leave, shift, statutory and incentive rules, run one month side by side with Excel, approve the first run, then switch Excel off.

Does Jwero handle hiring, onboarding and exit for jewellery staff?

Jwero keeps one record per person from offer to full and final: recruitment, onboarding tasks and documents, verification consent, appraisals, and a worked-out full and final settlement.

Does Jwero keep statutory registers for a jewellery business?

Jwero keeps statutory registers and prepares PF, ESI, PT and muster exports, so registers are not kept on paper.

Can I ask HR questions in plain words in Jwero?

Jwero lets you ask HR questions in plain words, such as who was late most this month, and gives a daily HR brief alongside HR reports.
