More questions jewellers ask.

What does Jwero’s jewellery HR & payroll software do?

HR and payroll software for jewellery shops: biometric and face-scanner attendance, payroll with PF, ESI, PT and TDS, sales incentives from real sales, payslips on WhatsApp, and a staff app in multiple languages. It is part of Jwero One: ₹18,000 a month, every module included, with the first month at ₹3,600.

Which HR software suits a jewellery business?

One built for showroom staff: attendance, leave, shifts, payroll with PF and ESI, sales incentives and payslips on WhatsApp. Jwero does this on the same record as sales; karigar wages are settled in manufacturing.

How do I calculate sales incentives in a jewellery shop?

Set the rule once, for example a percentage of sales above a target, and incentives are worked out from the bills each salesperson actually made. They flow into payroll with no separate sheet.

Which payroll software suits a jewellery business?

Look for attendance-aware payroll with PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS, plus sales incentives from real sales. Karigar wages are settled in Jwero’s manufacturing module. Jwero produces payslips, a bank file and the statutory export files; filing is still done by your accountant.

How do I hire sales staff for a jewellery showroom?

Track each candidate through applied, screening, interview, offer and hired in one pipeline. A hired candidate moves straight into onboarding with a checklist, so documents and training are not forgotten.

How do I assign daily tasks to jewellery staff?

Each person gets a list of what to do today: follow-ups due, stock to count, visits expected. Tasks can be assigned by a manager and are tracked to completion.

What is HR and payroll software for a jewellery shop?

Software that records attendance, works out salaries with PF, ESI, PT and TDS, pays sales incentives, and keeps staff records from hiring to exit. Jwero does this on the same record as your sales.

How do I track staff attendance in a jewellery shop?

With biometric or face-scanner devices, or punches from a phone or kiosk with geo-fencing and a selfie. Late punches can be regularised and leave is counted automatically.

How are sales incentives calculated?

From each person’s real sales or margin, on the slabs you set, with clawback if a sale is returned. Try the calculator on this page.

Do staff get payslips on WhatsApp?

Yes. Payslips go to each staff member on WhatsApp, and are also in the staff app.

Can staff use the app in their language?

Yes. Staff self-service screens are available in multiple languages.

Does the bank file pay people?

It prepares a ready-to-upload bank file; a person uploads it in your banking portal.

Does this replace my accountant?

No. PF, ESI, PT and TDS are worked out and the files prepared, and your accountant files them.

Who can see salary data?

Seeing, editing and approving payroll are separate permissions, so a manager can approve leave without seeing salaries.

Does it pay karigars?

Karigar wages are settled in the manufacturing module, by piece, weight or day.
