More questions jewellers ask.

How do jewellers send WhatsApp broadcasts without getting banned?

Send through the official WhatsApp Business API using approved templates, only to people who agreed, and honour opt-outs. Jwero skips anyone who opted out and shows why, which is what keeps a number safe.

Do push notifications work for jewellery stores?

They work for visitors who opted in on your website or app: a new collection, a rate drop, a scheme reminder. They cost nothing per message, and go out from the same campaign as WhatsApp and email.

Does SMS marketing still work for jewellers?

SMS is best for short, certain messages such as payment received, order ready or instalment due. For offers, WhatsApp usually gets more response. Both can go out from one campaign.

What is a Broadcast?

A single send, WhatsApp, email, SMS or push, to an audience built from a segment, a group or a tag. WhatsApp sends are template-linked, following the same approved-template discipline as the rest of Jwero’s WhatsApp story.

What does "consent-aware" actually mean?

A delivery automatically skips a recipient who has opted out, is on DND, or is missing a required template, a structured skip-reason system, not a manual checklist someone has to remember to run.

What is a Campaign, and how is it different from a Broadcast?

A campaign wraps one or more broadcasts and journeys with UTM-based attribution and a reporting layer, so you see what a campaign actually sold, not just how many messages went out.

Where does the audience for a broadcast or campaign come from?

From segments, built in Jwero’s segmentation tool, or from groups and tags. The same segments feed both journeys and campaigns.

Does the AI write and send campaigns on its own?

No. The AI campaign-strategist turns a brief into a draft plan, audience, message angles, timing, but a person still reviews, builds and sends it. Nothing goes out without your team’s action, the same "AI drafts, human approves" rule used across Jwero.

Can a campaign target a loyalty tier?

Yes, loyalty tiers are one of the ways to build an audience, so a campaign can target your top tier specifically rather than everyone at once.
