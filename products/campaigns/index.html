More questions jewellers ask.

What does Jwero’s jewellery marketing software do?

Jewellery marketing software: festival campaigns planned by AI, sent on WhatsApp, RCS, SMS, email and push to live segments, with A/B testing, coupons and sales traced to each campaign. It is part of Jwero One: ₹18,000 a month, every module included, with the first month at ₹3,600.

How do jewellers send WhatsApp broadcasts without getting banned?

Send through the official WhatsApp Business API using approved templates, only to people who agreed, and honour opt-outs. Jwero skips anyone who opted out and shows why, which is what keeps a number safe.

Do push notifications work for jewellery stores?

They work for visitors who opted in on your website or app: a new collection, a rate drop, a scheme reminder. They cost nothing per message, and go out from the same campaign as WhatsApp and email.

Does SMS marketing still work for jewellers?

SMS is best for short, certain messages such as payment received, order ready or instalment due. For offers, WhatsApp usually gets more response. Both can go out from one campaign.

What is jewellery marketing software?

Software that plans and sends a jeweller’s campaigns across WhatsApp, RCS, SMS, email and push to the right customers, with offers like coupons and gift vouchers, and shows what each campaign sold.

How should a jeweller plan festival campaigns?

Plan from the calendar three weeks ahead: Akshaya Tritiya, Dhanteras, Diwali, wedding season and launches. Pick the audience for each, test two versions of the message, send on the channels each customer uses, and measure visits and bills.

Which marketing channels work best for jewellers?

WhatsApp for most customers, RCS and SMS for those not on WhatsApp, email and push for online customers. Jwero sends each customer on the channels they agreed to.

Can I A/B test a campaign?

Yes. Send two versions to a small share of the audience, and the version that performs better goes to everyone else.

How do jewellers measure what a campaign sold?

By tracing visits and bills back to the campaign and channel that reached the customer. Jwero shows revenue by campaign and by channel, not just opens.

Can AI plan and write jewellery campaigns?

Yes. The AI campaign strategist drafts the plan, audience, message and offer ahead of each festival. Nothing is sent until your team approves it.

Do campaigns include ads and social media posts?

No. Ads and social posts run from their own tools in Jwero, using the same segments, so the people you target online match the ones you message.

Is RCS available?

Yes. RCS rich messages are live for campaigns, for customers on Android phones who are not reachable on WhatsApp.

What is the difference between a campaign and a journey?

A campaign goes to an audience at a planned time, like a Diwali offer. A journey runs for each customer on their own timeline, like an anniversary or an abandoned cart.
