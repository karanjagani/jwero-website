QUESTIONS

What people ask before they message.

What is Personalised Promotions?

Personalised Promotions is how Jwero decides who hears what, where, and what it sold. Live segments come from purchases, gold plans, occasions and engagement. One promotion is personalised for each customer and sent as a WhatsApp broadcast or a campaign on SMS, email, push and AI calls, at each customer’s best time, with Meta, Google and Pinterest ads alongside. Campaigns can test two versions, offers attached, and every bill traced back to the promotion and channel.

What is the best WhatsApp marketing software for jewellers?

The best WhatsApp marketing software for jewellers sends to live segments built from purchases, gold plans and occasions, personalises each message, respects consent and frequency limits, and shows what each promotion sold. Jwero Personalised Promotions does all of this, with SMS, email, push, AI calls and ads alongside.

How do I send WhatsApp broadcasts to my jewellery customers?

Choose a segment, pick an approved template, personalise it with her name, occasion and today’s gold rate, then schedule it. Jwero sends each message at the customer’s usual reading time, checks consent and frequency caps, and can follow up those who did not respond.

How should jewellers segment customers?

By what they bought, their metal and spend, how recently they bought, gold plans, birthdays and anniversaries, location and engagement. Jwero keeps segments live, offers 41 ready ones, includes RFM, and turns plain words into a segment.

When should jewellers start Diwali marketing?

About three weeks ahead: build the segments, test two versions of the message, and send in the week before Dhanteras. Jwero suggests festival promotions in advance and shows when each one should go out.

How do jewellers measure campaign ROI?

By tracing visits and bills to the campaign and channel that reached the customer. Jwero reports revenue, cost and return by promotion and channel, and sends sales back to Meta and Google.

Do click-to-WhatsApp ads work for jewellery shops?

They suit jewellery well, because customers want to ask about a piece before visiting. Jwero turns a segment into a Meta audience, drafts the ad from your catalogue, and puts every click into One Inbox.

Can jewellers run Meta, Google and Pinterest ads from one place?

Yes. Jwero publishes to Meta, Google Search and Performance Max, and Pinterest promoted pins once you approve, with budget alerts and return on spend from real bills.

How do I stop customers getting too many messages?

Set limits. Jwero applies frequency caps over 7 and 30 days, quiet hours, DND checks and consent on every promotion, so no customer hears from you too often.

Can I A/B test a WhatsApp campaign?

Yes. In Jwero campaigns and journeys, two versions go to a small share of the audience and the better one goes to everyone else.

What should a jeweller send on a customer’s anniversary?

A personal note that names the occasion and shows pieces that go with what she bought before. Jwero’s anniversary segment and journeys send it at the right time, with an offer if you choose.

How do I bring back customers who have not bought in a year?

Use a “quiet for 12 months” segment, high value first, with a personal note about her last purchase and a reason to visit. Jwero can send it on WhatsApp, SMS, email or an AI call.

Can AI write jewellery promotions?

Yes. Jwero’s AI turns a brief into a campaign, suggests festival promotions, writes broadcast copy and drafts ads from your catalogue. You choose what goes out on its own and what waits for approval.

Which channels should a jeweller use for promotions?

WhatsApp first, then SMS, email, push and AI calls for customers who prefer them, with ads to reach new buyers. Jwero sends each customer on the channels she agreed to.

How much does WhatsApp marketing software for jewellers cost?

Jwero starts with a free trial that includes every module; your price is shown inside your account after the trial. WhatsApp message charges are paid to Meta separately.
