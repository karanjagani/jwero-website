More questions jewellers ask.

What does Jwero’s jewellery catalogue & PIM software do?

Jewellery catalogue management software and PIM: one record per piece, priced at today’s gold rate, AI listings from photos, bulk import from Excel, Shopify or WooCommerce, and automatic sync to your WhatsApp catalogue, Google Shopping, Meta, POS, ecommerce and marketplaces. It is part of Jwero One: ₹18,000 a month, every module included, with the first month at ₹3,600.

Where do I store jewellery photos and videos?

In the asset library, attached to the piece they belong to, so the same photo serves the catalogue, WhatsApp shares, the website and social posts. Google Drive, OneDrive and Dropbox can be connected.

What is jewellery PIM?

Jewellery PIM (product information management) is one master record for every piece, with metal, purity, weights, stones, certificates, photos and descriptions, that feeds every place you sell, from the counter to the website, WhatsApp, Google Shopping and marketplaces.

What is jewellery catalogue software?

Software that keeps your jewellery catalogue in one place, prices every piece from today’s gold rate, and publishes it to your website, WhatsApp, POS, mobile app and marketplaces, so the same piece shows the same price everywhere.

How do I create product listings from photos?

Add the photo. Jwero’s AI reads the type of piece and writes the description, and the weights, purity and stones come from your stock record. Your team checks and publishes.

How do I sync jewellery prices to Shopify, Google Shopping and Meta?

Jwero recalculates each price from today’s rate and syncs automatically to your website, Shopify or WooCommerce, Google Shopping, Meta catalogues, POS, mobile apps and marketplaces whenever the rate or the product changes.

Can it handle one-of-a-kind pieces?

Yes. Custom fields record provenance, unique certificates and the story of each piece, where a standard template does not fit.

Can the catalogue handle certificates and hallmarking details?

Yes. Purity, stone details, certificate numbers and hallmark ID are structured fields you can search and filter, not free text.

Can I share a catalogue without showing all my stock?

Yes. Share chosen pieces as a private live link, decide whether prices show, and see who viewed what. Enquiries turn into quotes, and customers can pay inside the catalogue.

I have thousands of products. Will setup take forever?

No. We import in bulk from Excel, Shopify, WooCommerce, a software export or photo folders, and AI fills missing details for your team to check.

Does my catalogue show inside WhatsApp?

Yes. The Meta sync fills your WhatsApp Business catalogue, so customers browse pieces inside the chat, priced at today’s rate and removed when sold.

Can the catalogue connect to my other software?

Yes. Bulk import from Excel, Shopify and WooCommerce, and APIs and webhooks to keep other systems in step.

What is catalogue management software for jewellers?

Catalog management software (also spelt catalogue) keeps every piece in one record and publishes it to every channel. For jewellers it also prices from the gold rate and stores purity, stones, certificates and hallmark ID. Jwero does this as its catalogue and PIM module.

Can AI create jewellery product photos?

Jwero can generate or edit a product image from a product photo, charged per image from the wallet. It does not create virtual try-on images.
