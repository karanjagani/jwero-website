QUESTIONS

What people ask before they message.

What does Jwero’s digital catalogue app for jewellers do?

Digital jewellery catalogues: share an online catalogue on WhatsApp at today’s gold rate, hide prices or password protect it, give B2B buyers their own pricing, see who viewed what, and take quotes and advance payments. It is part of Jwero One: ₹18,000 a month, every module included, with the first month at ₹3,600.

What is a digital jewellery catalogue?

A live link of chosen pieces you share instead of a PDF. With Jwero it prices at today’s rate, shows who viewed what, and lets the customer request a quote or pay on the link.

How do I share a jewellery catalogue on WhatsApp?

Pick the pieces, and Jwero creates the link with a caption written for you. Send it to one customer or a whole segment.

Can I see who viewed my catalogue?

Yes. Opens, the pieces viewed and the time spent are written to the customer’s record, and your team is notified when she opens it.

Can I hide prices?

Yes. Show prices at today’s rate, hide them, or show them on request.

Can a catalogue be private?

Yes. Protect a link with a password so only the people you choose can open it.

Can wholesale buyers get their own prices?

Yes. Each B2B buyer link carries its own pricing and terms.

Why not just send a PDF?

A PDF has yesterday’s prices, can be forwarded anywhere, and tells you nothing. A link stays at today’s rate, can be private, and reports back.

How is this different from the Catalogue (PIM)?

The catalogue holds every piece once. A digital catalogue is a chosen set from it, shared with one customer, family, buyer or season.
