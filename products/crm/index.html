QUESTIONS

What people ask before they message.

What is Jwero CRM?

Jwero CRM is a customer system built for jewellers. It captures leads from 20+ sources automatically, matches them to existing customers and households, gives each an owner and a reply clock, and keeps purchases, gold plans, occasions and every chat and call on one record. Scores and a daily list tell the team who to call; journeys, AI calls and Meta audiences engage and retarget; consent and data requests are handled on the record.

What is the best CRM for jewellers in 2026?

The best jewellery CRM captures leads from every channel on its own, knows families, gold plans, purchases and occasions, tells each salesperson who to call today, and keeps consent and data requests in order. Jwero CRM does all of this on one record shared with billing, chat and calls.

What is a jewellery CRM?

A jewellery CRM is customer software built for how jewellery is bought: it keeps households, gold plan balances, purchases, occasions and every chat and call on one record, and tells the team who to contact and why.

What is the best WhatsApp CRM for jewellers?

A WhatsApp CRM for jewellers puts every WhatsApp chat on the customer’s record next to her purchases, gold plan and occasions. Jwero CRM does this for WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, webchat and calls, gives each new chat an owner and a reply clock, and starts a follow-up journey on its own.

Which lead sources can a jewellery CRM capture automatically?

Jwero CRM captures 20+ sources on its own, including WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger DMs, social comments, website chat and forms, Meta and Google lead forms, Justdial, online store sign-ins, product questions, showroom visits, phone and AI calls, email, video meetings, catalogue views and counter bills.

How do jewellers stop losing leads?

Jewellers stop losing leads by capturing every enquiry automatically, giving it an owner at once and timing the first reply. Jwero CRM routes each lead to a branch and salesperson, starts a reply clock with breach alerts, and starts a journey so nobody waits.

How do I manage walk-in customers in a jewellery showroom?

Check each walk-in in by phone number so their record opens with past purchases, gold plan and occasions, log the pieces they try, and follow up the ones who leave. Jwero CRM captures showroom visits as a source and links the later bill to the visit.

How do I avoid duplicate customers across WhatsApp, Instagram and the counter?

Jwero CRM matches a customer across channels and merges duplicates, and lets one phone number belong to several family members, so the record stays single and correct whether she messaged, commented or bought at the counter.

How do jewellers increase repeat customers?

Jewellers increase repeat customers by remembering occasions, keeping gold plan members engaged to maturity, rewarding loyalty and reaching customers who are drifting away. Jwero CRM finds occasions, scores churn risk and puts the right customers on each morning’s list.

How do I remember customer birthdays and anniversaries?

Jwero CRM finds birthdays and anniversaries from the customer’s record and purchases, lists who has an occasion coming weeks ahead, and can start a personal message or a call reminder for each one.

What scores does a jewellery CRM give each customer?

Jwero CRM keeps intent, conversion, engagement, relationship health, churn risk, opportunity, revenue potential, trust risk, RFM segment, value tier, next occasion and best time to message, plus a next best action for each customer.

Who should my salespeople call today?

Jwero CRM builds each salesperson’s daily list from scores, occasions, open quotes, maturing gold plans and reply clocks, sends the owner a morning digest, and drafts the follow-up message with AI so it can go in one tap.

Can a CRM send leads to Meta ads for retargeting?

Jwero CRM segments sync to Meta as custom audiences, so ads reach the customers and enquiries your team is already working, and the audience updates as people move in and out of the segment.

Can I ask my CRM questions in plain words?

Jwero lets you type questions such as “bridal enquiries from Surat this month that did not visit” and returns the answer from your own customers, bills and conversations.

How do I stop staff taking customer lists when they leave?

Keep customers in the shop’s system, not on staff phones. In Jwero CRM every chat and call sits on the customer record, sensitive fields are limited by role, and security and role changes are recorded in audit logs.

Is customer data safe in a jewellery CRM?

Jwero CRM keeps a consent log per channel, handles customer requests to export or erase data, masks personal details, sets rules for what AI may use, limits sensitive fields by role, and records security and role changes in audit logs.

Do jewellers need customer consent before marketing in India?

Jewellers should record consent before marketing and honour requests to see or delete personal data. Jwero CRM keeps consent per channel as a history and handles export and erasure requests; confirm your obligations with your advisor.

Can several family members share one phone number in a CRM?

Jwero CRM links a household and lets one phone number belong to several people, so the bride, her mother and the father who pays are each recognised and their purchases stay on the right record.

Should a jewellery shop use Excel or a CRM?

Excel works for a short list, but it cannot capture leads on its own, merge duplicates, remember occasions, time replies or tell staff who to call. Jwero CRM does these on the same record as billing, chat and calls.

Is Jwero better than Zoho CRM or Salesforce for jewellers?

Zoho CRM and Salesforce know names, notes and deals. Jwero CRM already knows gold plans, purchases, households, occasions, loyalty and every WhatsApp message and call, because billing, chat and calling share one system.

Is there a Zithara alternative for jewellers?

Jwero CRM is an alternative to Zithara that keeps the customer record on the same platform as billing, stock, gold plans, WhatsApp, ads and the online store. The Jwero vs Zithara comparison sets out what to check on your own data.

Can I import my existing customer list into a jewellery CRM?

Jwero imports customer lists from Excel, CSV, phone contacts or other software. During onboarding the team cleans the list, merges duplicates, links households and brings purchases, notes and occasions across.

How much does a jewellery CRM cost?

Jwero CRM starts with a free trial that includes every module, so you can try it on your own customers first; your price is shown inside your account after the trial.
