More questions jewellers ask.

What does Jwero’s jewellery ads manager do?

Jewellery ads manager: Meta and Google ads from one place, click-to-WhatsApp ads into your team inbox, audiences from your customers, AI image and video creatives, budget caps, and sales reported back. It is part of Jwero One, the single plan with every module included. You start with a free trial, and your price is shown in your account when it ends.

Can I advertise my jewellery shop on ChatGPT?

Advertising inside AI assistants is new and changing. Jwero’s Ads Manager covers Meta, Google and Pinterest today; ask us about ChatGPT ads for your account before planning a budget around them.

Do Google Ads work for jewellery shops?

They work when someone is already searching, such as “gold bangles near me”, and when the enquiry is answered quickly. Jwero builds Search, Performance Max and Shopping campaigns and shows which ad led to a sale.

What is the best way to advertise a jewellery shop?

Ads that start a conversation usually work best for jewellery: click-to-WhatsApp ads on Instagram and Facebook, Google Search for people looking for a jeweller nearby, and Google Shopping with prices that follow the gold rate. Target your own customers and people like them, and judge ads by the bills they produce.

Do click-to-WhatsApp ads work for jewellers?

They suit jewellery well, because customers want to ask about a piece and a price before visiting. Jwero runs them so each click becomes a chat in your team inbox, answered with pieces priced at today’s rate.

How do jewellers measure return on ad spend?

By tracing ads to chats, visits and bills, not clicks. Jwero reports the rupees of sales per rupee spent and sends counter and online sales back to Meta and Google, so the platforms find more real buyers.

Should I use an agency or run ads myself?

Run them yourself in Jwero, with AI making the creatives and your budget capped, or let Jwero’s team run them for you. Either way the ads, chats and sales stay on your own records.

How much should a jeweller spend on ads?

Start small, with a daily cap, on one audience and one ad type, measure the bills it brings, and increase only what pays. There is no right number without your own results.

Which ad platforms does Jwero cover?

Meta (Instagram and Facebook, including Advantage+ and lead forms) and Google (Search, Performance Max and Shopping), published directly once you approve. Pinterest publishing is rolling out.

Can AI make jewellery ad creatives?

Yes. AI makes images, ad copy and short video clips from your catalogue photos; you edit and approve them before they run.

Can the AI spend my budget without me knowing?

No. Nothing publishes without your approval, every campaign has a cap, you get alerts as spend grows, and one switch stops it all.

Does this replace my existing ad accounts?

No. Your Meta and Google accounts stay yours; Jwero connects to them.
