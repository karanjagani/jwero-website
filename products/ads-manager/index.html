ONE WORKSPACE, THREE PLATFORMS

Stop juggling three ad managers.

Meta, Google & Pinterest

Each platform has its own integration and its own catalogue of supported campaign types, built for that platform, not a lowest-common-denominator form.

Automatic publishing to Meta & Google

Approve a campaign in the wizard and Jwero calls the Meta Graph API and Google Ads API directly to create the live budget, campaign, ad sets and creatives, not a draft you copy-paste in elsewhere.

Google Search, Performance Max & Shopping

Google campaigns cover Search (with server-side checks, e.g. at least three headlines before it lets you submit), Performance Max, and Shopping campaigns linked to your jewellery catalogue.

Meta Advantage+ & lead-gen forms

Meta campaigns include Advantage+ automated campaigns and native lead-gen ad forms, alongside standard campaign types.

Create/edit wizard

One guided flow to build and adjust a campaign, instead of relearning three different ad-platform interfaces.

Budget alerts

An automated monitoring worker watches spend and flags a campaign as it approaches its limit, before the spend can surprise you.

Approval before spend

Every campaign passes an approval step in the wizard before Jwero submits it to the ad platform. Nobody spends the marketing budget by accident.

AI opportunity analysis

The AI strategist surfaces where a campaign is underperforming or where there’s budget headroom worth using, based on your performance data, a draft recommendation you review, not generative copywriting. It doesn’t write your ad headlines or body copy; those are still entered by hand in the wizard.

Learnings dashboards

Performance and learnings in one view across platforms, so you compare Meta against Google against Pinterest without exporting three reports.

ADS MANAGER QUESTIONS

Budget control, publishing, and what the AI does.

What does Jwero’s jewellery ads manager do?

Create ad campaigns across Meta, Google and Pinterest from one place, approve in the wizard and Jwero publishes straight to Meta and Google, with budget alerts and an approval step before spend. It is part of Jwero One: ₹18,000 a month, every module included, with the first month at ₹3,600.

Does Jwero publish my campaign straight to the ad platform?

Yes, for Meta and Google, approve a campaign in the wizard and Jwero submits it directly via the Meta Graph API and Google Ads API to create the budget, campaign, ad sets and creatives. Pinterest publishing is still rolling out.

Can the AI spend my budget without me knowing?

No, it drafts opportunity analysis and learnings, not ad copy, and never spends. Every campaign needs your approval in the wizard before Jwero calls the ad platform.

Which platforms are covered?

Meta Ads, Google Ads and Pinterest. Google spans Search, Performance Max and Shopping; Meta spans standard campaigns, Advantage+ and lead-gen forms.

More questions? See the full FAQ →

More questions jewellers ask.

Can I advertise my jewellery shop on ChatGPT?

Advertising inside AI assistants is new and changing. Jwero’s Ads Manager covers Meta, Google and Pinterest today; ask us about ChatGPT ads for your account before planning a budget around them.

Do Google Ads work for jewellery shops?

They work when someone is already searching, such as “gold bangles near me”, and when the enquiry is answered quickly. Jwero builds Search, Performance Max and Shopping campaigns and shows which ad led to a sale.

Which ad platforms does Jwero manage?

Meta Ads, Google Ads and Pinterest, each with its own integration and campaign-type catalogue. Google covers Search, Performance Max and Shopping (linked to your jewellery catalogue); Meta covers standard campaigns, Advantage+ automated campaigns and lead-gen ad forms.

What happens if I go over budget?

An automated monitoring worker watches spend and alerts you as campaigns approach their limits, so overspend is something you catch early, not something you discover on the invoice.

How is this different from managing ads inside Meta or Google directly?

You get one wizard for campaign creation across all three platforms, one place for budget alerts and approvals, and, for Meta and Google, automatic publishing straight from that wizard, instead of three separate ad managers with three separate logins.

Does this replace my existing ad accounts?

No, it connects to and manages your existing Meta, Google and Pinterest ad accounts from inside Jwero, rather than replacing them.
