QUESTIONS MANUFACTURERS ASK

What manufacturers ask about job-work and loss.

What does Jwero’s jewellery manufacturing software do?

Work-in-progress tracking with per-stage gold-loss norms, artisan job-work control, assay-verified intake, and B2B catalogue distribution to retail buyers. It is part of Jwero One, the single plan with every module included. You start with a free trial, and your price is shown in your account when it ends.

Will karigars resist being tracked more closely?

Think job cards, rather than surveillance: the same records that catch abnormal loss also settle disputes in the karigar’s favour. It protects both sides.

Our process is unusual: casting, CAD, export-specific. Does this fit?

See casting units, CAD services or export houses for your exact fit.

Does it handle karigar TDS?

Yes. Karigars have a directory with their details, and a TDS screen for deductions on their labour payments. Settlements bring together metal, wastage and labour for each karigar.

Can I control stone loss at setting?

Stones are issued from lots by carat, and stone loss is checked at finished goods against what was issued.

Can I define the process each design goes through?

Yes. Routing templates set the stages, and bills of material set the metal and stones, for each design. Production orders then follow those stages, so you see work in progress by stage.

Can Jwero track gold loss per production stage?

Yes, an append-only work-in-progress ledger tracks fine weight through every stage with per-stage loss norms; abnormal loss is flagged the day it happens, not at year-end stocktake.

Does it handle artisan job-work?

Yes, job-work issue and receipt with weight reconciliation, gated by the rules you set per artisan and order.

Can wholesalers take orders on WhatsApp?

Yes, share live B2B catalogues with retailer-specific visibility, take orders in chat, and track the whole purchase-to-pay chain.

Is karigar wage settlement included?

Yes: karigar wage and payroll settlement, rate cards, work logs, khata ledger, settlement runs, is shipped, alongside job-work tracking itself (issue, receipt, weight reconciliation).

Will karigars resist being tracked more closely than the notebook they’re used to?

Frame it as job cards, not surveillance, the same records that catch abnormal loss also settle disputes in the karigar’s favour when they did nothing wrong. Job tracking protects both sides, not just the owner.

Our process is unusual: casting, CAD or export-specific. Does this fit?

Casting, CAD and export-house specific pages exist because these workflows genuinely differ, see casting units, CAD services or export houses for your exact fit rather than a generic answer.
