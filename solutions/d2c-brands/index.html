QUESTIONS D2C BRANDS ASK

Shopify, trials and the stack you keep, answered.

Do I have to leave Shopify?

No, the Shopify connector keeps your store on the same product data, so Jwero adds WhatsApp, Instagram and memory on top of the store you already run.

Is there a free trial for D2C brands?

No free trial, but the first month is ₹3,600 instead of ₹18,000. Create a workspace in three steps at /start, connect Shopify or WooCommerce, and evaluate on your own orders. No demo required.

What happens to my Shopify data if I leave Jwero?

Nothing happens to Shopify, it stays the store of record for your storefront and is never modified by a disconnect. Jwero holds synced copies of orders and customers plus everything it added (WhatsApp threads, scores, occasions). All of it exports as CSV before or after you leave, and the connector is removed from Shopify in one click.

We already use a lot of ecommerce tools. Won’t this just be one more?

It replaces the gap between them, not the tools themselves, the WhatsApp/Instagram-native selling and live-rate pricing a generic ecommerce stack doesn’t do, added on top of what you keep.

More questions jewellers ask.

Do I need Shopify, or can Jwero run my store?

Either. Jwero has its own store with domains, design and orders, or you can connect Shopify or WooCommerce and keep stock in sync. Store orders, abandoned carts and landing pages are all managed in Jwero.

Can I see what visitors do on my website?

Yes. Website analytics and heatmaps show which pages and products visitors look at, and forms and chat capture their numbers. You can also test two offer banners and see which one gets more responses.

Can customers ask questions on WhatsApp and get answers at night?

Yes. An AI assistant answers from what you teach it, within limits you set, and staff see every conversation in the inbox. You set what it may and may not promise, such as discounts.
