USE CASES

Where this pays off in a jewellery business.

Four everyday situations, and what changes when Jwero handles them.

01

Billing a 22K chain means working out rate, weight and making charges.

Scan the tag and the bill fills in net weight, purity, today's rate, making charge and GST, ready to print.

What changesA gold bill takes a scan, not a calculator.

02

A customer brings her old bangles to exchange for a new necklace.

Record the old gold's weight and purity at the counter, value it at today's rate, and set it off against the new bill.

What changesOld gold exchange is on the bill, not on a separate slip.

03

Wedding season is near and customers want a monthly gold saving plan.

Create an instalment scheme with your bonus rules, enrol customers, take payments at the desk and see who has missed an instalment.

What changesThe desk knows who to call about missed instalments.

04

A customer needs cash today against her gold chain.

Gold loan record the pledged pieces, weight and value, work out interest and handle renewal or release.

What changesLoan interest is worked out by the system, not by hand.

QUESTIONS GOLD RETAILERS ASK

What gold retailers ask about rate-linked pricing.

What does Jwero’s gold jewellery shop software do?

Gold jewellery shop software: live-rate pricing, gold scheme enrolment and old-gold exchange in one flow, for a business where the rate keeps moving. It is part of Jwero One, the single plan with every module included. You start with a free trial, and your price is shown in your account when it ends.

How does pricing stay accurate as the rate moves?

Catalogue prices are formulas: rate × weight × purity plus making charges, resolved live wherever the product appears. Change the rate once; everything follows.

Can I run my gold savings scheme alongside daily selling?

Yes, schemes, savings plans and everyday catalogue selling share the same customer record and pricing engine.

What if we mis-price something because the rate updated mid-conversation?

Prices resolve live at the moment they’re shown or invoiced, not cached from earlier in the day, that specific risk is what rate-linked pricing is built to remove.

How do you handle hallmark ID on gold pieces?

Every piece can store its hallmark ID and it prints on the tag and the bill. The hallmarking screen tracks batches sent for hallmarking and what has come back.

Can I set different making charges for different designs?

Yes. Making charges can be per gram, a percentage or a fixed amount, set by category or per design, and the price updates with the day's rate.

Do you show how much I owe customers in gold schemes?

There is a scheme report showing what each enrolled customer has paid and what is due at maturity. The desk sees who to call, and reminders go out on their own.
