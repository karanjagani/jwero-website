Successful jewellery marketing is not about running random campaigns.

The most successful jewellery retailers plan their marketing activities throughout the year.

A structured marketing calendar helps businesses:

  • Increase customer engagement
  • Improve campaign performance
  • Generate consistent leads
  • Maximize festive opportunities
  • Increase sales throughout the year

This guide provides a month-by-month jewellery marketing calendar.

Why Every Jewellery Store Needs a Marketing Calendar

Without planning, businesses often:

  • Miss key sales opportunities
  • Launch campaigns too late
  • Create inconsistent customer engagement
  • Waste marketing budgets

A marketing calendar creates consistency and predictability.

Benefits of a Jewellery Marketing Calendar

  • Better Campaign Planning

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is a marketing calendar important for jewellers?

A marketing calendar is important for jewellers because it helps businesses plan campaigns, maximise seasonal opportunities, and maintain consistent customer engagement.

Which months are most important for jewellery marketing?

Akshaya Tritiya, wedding season months, Diwali, and year-end gifting periods.

Should WhatsApp be part of a marketing calendar?

Yes. WhatsApp should be a part of a marketing calendar because it supports customer engagement, promotions, and appointment booking throughout the year.

How does CRM help marketing planning?

CRM improves customer segmentation, campaign execution, tracking, and follow-ups.

How often should jewellers run campaigns?

Most successful retailers engage customers consistently every month.

What metrics should be tracked?

Metrics like Leads, appointments, conversions, revenue, repeat sales, and campaign ROI should be tracked.
